## Problem addressed
The specification says there is a need for a quicker, cheaper way to perform and permanently record day-to-day exchanges of any entity of value, not only cryptocurrency. Known blockchain exchanges are described as still relying on traditional models, third-party sources, escrows and multi-currency processors, achieving decentralisation through business arrangements rather than cryptographic technique.

## Core mechanism
Users publish invitations (offers or requests) whose metadata state the entities to be exchanged and conditions such as rates, quantity bounds, deadline or location. Invitation details sit in a redeem script, preferably in the slots a protocol reserves for public keys, and a hash of the signed redeem script is published on a distributed hash table and used as a P2SH locking script on a blockchain transaction. A matching service scans the DHT for mirrored offer/request types and compatible conditions, then generates exchange transaction(s) that spend the invitation outputs. Tokens represent contracts (fiat, title, tickets, services) pegged or not to the underlying coin amount; an optional escrow co-signs the P2SH unlock. The invitation transaction itself may carry only dust or a deposit; goods-for-goods exchanges can be recorded without transferring value.

## Independent claims (gist — not legal scope)
Claim 1 is a method in which an exchange service receives invitation details, builds a redeem script that includes those details and the client public key, obtains a client-signed redeem script, publishes the details and the script hash on a DHT, builds a locking script from that hash, and broadcasts a blockchain transaction that spends a stated cryptocurrency amount to a P2SH script unlockable only with signatures of the client and an escrow provider. Claim 12 is apparatus configured to perform that method.

## BSV relevance
The embodiment depends on Bitcoin P2SH, metadata embedded where keys would sit, DHT-linked invitations and tokenised outputs. Those are concrete patterns for BSV token and marketplace transaction construction.
