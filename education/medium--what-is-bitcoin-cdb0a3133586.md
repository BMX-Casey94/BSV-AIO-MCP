# What is Bitcoin — core principles

- **Legal character follows transaction construction.** Depending on how it is structured, a Bitcoin transaction can be a contractual exchange, electronic cash, a promissory note or another financial security, a bill of sale, or a simple ledger entry — with regulatory consequences running from the coinbase allocation of rewards to the payment and consideration for an exchange.
- **Money is functional, not state-defined.** Per Miller v Race (1758) and Moss v Hancock (1899), money is what passes freely from hand to hand throughout the community in final discharge of debts, accepted equally without reference to the character or credit of the person offering it — it is common consent and conduct that gives a thing the character of money.
- **Bitcoin is ownable intangible property.** Being money, Bitcoin is an intangible property capable of being owned — even where its status as "currency" is less clear.
- **Currency means negotiability.** Per Emmett J in Travelex v Commissioner of Taxation, currency concerns the negotiability of money: title passes by mere delivery, exempt from nemo dat quod non habet, so the recipient need not enquire into the title of the payer (David Fox, Property Rights in Money).
- **Courts already treat Bitcoin as money.** In SEC v Shavers (E.D. Tex.) the argument that a Bitcoin scheme involved no securities because "Bitcoin was not money" failed — US jurisprudence places Bitcoin within the ordinary meaning of "money" and perhaps "currency".
- **Legal uncertainty limits use.** Bitcoin has been classed as a virtual token, a currency or a security at different times and places within individual jurisdictions; that inconsistency — not government hostility — is itself a cause of its limited use.
- **State recognition is the expected trajectory.** German acceptance of Bitcoin as a unit of account and the postulate before the Swiss Parliament to treat Bitcoin as foreign currency make formal recognition as currency by a government look inevitable.
- **Bitcoin is cash.** Whatever the classification puzzle, the system's design purpose is P2P electronic cash, analysed inside existing law rather than outside it.
