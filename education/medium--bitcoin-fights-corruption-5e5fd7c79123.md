# Bitcoin Fights Corruption — core principles

- **Bitcoin was designed to act within law.** The vision is a system that destroys crime, not one that avoids government.
- **The permanent record enables tracing.** SPV lets users act as peers with privacy, while the design removes anonymity. If you purchase drugs using Bitcoin, you will be recorded forever; ransomware proceeds will eventually be seized.
- **Immutable records make promoters accountable.** Earlier conmen escaped by destroying records. Bitcoin’s record means token creators can be tracked down a decade later.
- **Another token is not innovation.** Wealth comes from industry, goods, services and a company that delivers what it promises — not from issuing tokens.
- **Decentralisation is not an end in itself.** The call for decentralisation for its own sake does not make sense; it is not the purpose of Bitcoin.
- **Bitcoin creates trust; it does not destroy it.** Because crime is captured, fraud reversed and theft rendered worthless, a world based on trust can be built.
- **Honest money favours stewardship.** The system favours long-term, generational capitalism over short-term extraction — businesses designed to last generations, not quarters.
- **Global micropayments are inclusion.** Low-fee micropayments open global markets so people in poorer countries can earn small payments and help themselves.
