# The Lawless Blockchain Is a Story We Tell for Small Change — core principles

- **Protocol-only deterrence covers small payments; large transfers bring law back in.** Budish's condition — attack share × flow cost × duration must exceed the double-spend prize — is the no-law, no-pool special case. It is contained, not refuted.
- **The legal term in the attacker's payoff is a gradient, not a switch.** Detection × attribution × sanction landing × sanction size, minus the enforcer's cost, floored at zero. Law nobody will pay to use is worth nothing. The term is exactly zero for a five-dollar double-spend and rises with the prize, because disgorgement grows at least one-for-one while enforcement cost is largely fixed overhead.
- **Mining is a small set of identifiable operators, not an anonymous swarm.** Over 98% of hash power is attributable to publicly named pools. Attribution runs to the pool operator; exchanges, prosecutors and courts sit inside the real payoff matrix.
- **Pooled attacking capacity is a fragile coalition.** A covert attack degrades payouts and raises stale shares, so contributors leave. Once suspected, continued association brings legal scrutiny, exchange exclusion and hardware impairment. Effective capacity collapses within hours, far below majority.
- **Four further omitted losses raise the real cost of attack.** Reputational sanctions (fraud against identifiable victims), ASIC capital loss (specialised silicon converts a flow-cost problem into a stock-loss problem), honest-longest-chain exclusion, and network-friction loss.
- **Code is not law.** Security through mathematics and electricity alone is a romance untested by serious money. The real contrast is between different bundles of protocol incentive, organisational structure, legal exposure and infrastructural dependence.
- **A sensible division of labour follows.** Protocol incentives police the ocean of small payments; protocol-plus-law deters large transfers through identifiable operators. The lawless blockchain is a story we tell for small change.
