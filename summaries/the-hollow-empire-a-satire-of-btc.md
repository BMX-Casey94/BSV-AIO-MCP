# The Hollow Empire: A Satire of BTC Core as a Parasitic Bureaucracy Masquerading as Innovation
**Date:** 2025-09-27 | **URL:** https://singulargrit.substack.com/p/the-hollow-empire-a-satire-of-btc
**Subtitle:** How managerial theatre, protocol capture, and empty technocracy replaced the substance of digital cash

## Core thesis
BTC Core is satirised as a parasitic clerical order — a self-perpetuating bureaucracy that feeds on Bitcoin's reputation while producing nothing. Its committees ritualise non-decision, its "consensus" is circular gatekeeping, the Lightning Network is a Kafkaesque side-show whose failures guarantee continued employment, and its cult of protocol conservatism has rebranded stagnation as virtue. The empire is hollow: measured against the hard test of utility — fast, cheap, reliable payments — the masquerade collapses.

## Key arguments and claims
- "The invention of stagnation": the pivot to "store of value" was "a calculated act of bureaucratic survival". Unable or unwilling to meet the engineering demands of scaling, Core redefined its failures as policy — block limits became "sacred parameters", congestion "proof of success" — sustained by incantatory slogans: "digital gold", "immutable protocol", "sound money".
- Committees in disguise: proposals are processed through layers of developers, reviewers and maintainers in perpetual deliberation that produces "activity without output, motion without movement". Gatekeeping is circular — "consensus exists because the consensus-makers declare it so" — likened to medieval councils debating angels on pinheads while the cathedral crumbles.
- Lightning as innovation theatre: it "exists only because BTC Core refused to scale the base layer as designed". Routing across liquidity-dependent channels, channel opening/closing, rebalancing and monitoring turn a simple payment into "the nightmare of Kafka's court". Its flaws — liquidity shortages, routing failures, custodial hubs — are reframed as research opportunities, making it "a system that never works well enough to conclude, but always badly enough to justify its continuation".
- The cult of conservatism: ossification is practised as religion, with the 1 MB limit as taboo and expansion proposals treated as heresy. The satire's sharpest doctrinal claim: "immutability was never the design. The protocol was meant to be set in stone only after it scaled to global capacity" — Core froze the cathedral mid-construction and worships the scaffolding.
- Parasites and hosts: Core "does not innovate, it does not build, it does not create utility" but drains the host's vitality while claiming credit for keeping it alive. An orbiting ecosystem of academics, influencers and self-styled experts lives as "managerial leeches" on the narrative. Narrative capture — seizing the name "Bitcoin" and rewriting it from digital cash to speculative commodity — is described as the parasite altering the host's behaviour to suit its diet.
- The masquerade of expertise: expert witnesses in legal disputes perform authority ("truth is not determined by fact but by the length of a CV"); jargon-dense papers and endless frameworks and reviews substitute for working systems — "a university department masquerading as an engineering team, producing citations in place of systems".
- A satirical case study casts Lightning developers as Kafka's clerks: a failed payment becomes a ticket, then meetings, frameworks and roadmaps "full of dragons and warnings but devoid of destinations"; failure is "not a problem but a resource".
- Collapse is inevitable on contact with reality: merchants and users need payments that are fast, cheap and reliable; the Soviet analogy is invoked — officials "proclaimed record harvests while the people queued for bread".

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Literary satire as critique: extended analogies (parasite biology, medieval scholasticism, Kafka's The Trial and The Castle, Soviet economic theatre) do the argumentative work by inversion — every claimed virtue (caution, conservatism, expertise) is recast as its opposite (fear, stagnation, pretence). The method is deliberately non-technical: it assumes the substantive case made elsewhere and attacks the institutional performance instead.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Scarcity-as-virtue is inverted: artificial block-space scarcity is obstruction rebranded, and "sound money" sloganeering substitutes incantation for utility.
- Conservatism-as-maturity is inverted: genuine immutability applies only after the protocol has scaled; freezing it early is "an abdication of responsibility".
- Lightning-as-scaling is inverted: true scaling means "accommodating millions of transactions directly on the ledger, accessible to all", not fragmenting flow into brittle private channels.
- Expertise-as-authority is inverted: credentials, peer review and frameworks are theatre; the only legitimate test is whether the system processes payments at scale.
- Consensus-as-legitimacy is inverted: Core's consensus is a closed circle of the anointed, contrasted with the open economic participation of the original design.

## Notable quotes
- "What BTC Core demonstrates is the bureaucrat's greatest trick: transforming the absence of progress into a badge of honour, and dressing paralysis in the garments of prudence."
- "The effect is circular: consensus exists because the consensus-makers declare it so."
- "It is the equivalent of freezing a cathedral halfway through construction and insisting the scaffolding itself is the masterpiece."
- "In truth, it is the opposite... they guard a corpse and call it a cathedral."
