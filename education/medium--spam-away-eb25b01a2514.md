# Spam Away… — core principles

- **Many small transactions are legitimate use, not spam.** The doctrine that sending lots of low-value payments is an attack is a false narrative used to justify small blocks, fee filters and dust limits.
- **Micropayments are cash behaviour.** On-chain Bitcoin is meant to carry high volumes of small payments; treating them as pollution inverts the cash design.
- **Quote lists are not protocol.** Circulating Satoshi maxims taken out of context are not a substitute for the actual design of the system.
- **Bitcoin is not an anti-government system.** No derivative blockchain will be either; builders who bet on anarchist cash will discover that the protocol sits inside the law.
- **Scale the base layer for volume.** Large blocks and high throughput of small transactions are the intended answer to the "spam" framing, not a retreat to off-chain substitutes.
