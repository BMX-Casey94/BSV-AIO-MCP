# Q&A/Written Interview — The answers — Part 3 — core principles

- **The 21M cap is pragmatic engineering, not mysticism.** It "allows a 2 week difficulty adjustment, 144 block daily reward and more. It fits in a 64bit integer and has enough range to be useful… No real magic, it is a good enough guess."
- **The other constants are equally contingent.** The 10-minute block target is simply a workable range (7–13 minutes); the 4-year halving cycle "works well in econometric systems as a medium-term figure"; the 50 BTC initial reward "could have been 60, or 48".
- **Proof-of-work's role is conservative stabilisation.** "The role of a PoW system is to make the system stable, it is conservative. Miners choose to get as many transactions into a block as possible, and the fees and other aspects are the result of an emergent process."
- **Post-scaling governance is a software market, not a reference client.** "Software houses will target miners and exchanges, and the best software will win" — a distributed, possibly microservices-based ecosystem of competing implementations.
- **Wealth is output, not bullion — the Spanish silver parable.** Despite American silver flooding in, Philip II declared bankruptcy three times (1557, 1575, 1596); bullion "created no industry nor growth", funded consumption and inflation, and enriched rivals such as the Dutch. Adam Smith: wealth is "the annual output of the necessaries, conveniences, and amusements of life".
- **A coin held idle is only a claim on future output.** Gold, printed fiat and any coin treated as a pure "store of value" are claims on the actual wealth of society — real wealth is goods and services, as resource-poor Singapore and 1970s–80s Japan proved through trade.
- **Use is the value driver.** "Use matters. Forget the HODL crap… Wealth is about creation of the means of production. It is not about a zero-sum game."
- **Fiat-backed tokens need sovereign-grade issuers.** A pegged token only works if issued by "a major Federal US bank or as a part of the US Treasury"; an unbacked "peg" is money creation, not a peg.
- **Scaling is an engineering programme with evidence.** Internal 300+GB block tests, the SSRN payment-infrastructure paper (abstract 3065857), compilers mapping high-level languages to Script, and Teranode under development; global adoption inflection hoped for around 2030–2034.
- **One chain survives at scale.** "There is no long term blockchain other than Bitcoin… One survives, all others end worth nothing"; permissioned blockchains are "just like personal internets. Utterly useless."
