# Forget anonymity. — core principles

- **Bitcoin was designed to work within existing law.** It is private but not anonymous, and needs no legislative change to survive AML and counter-terrorist-finance rules.
- **A cryptocurrency has no value without exchange.** Without merchant services, custodial wallets and payment systems, a coin cannot be used and therefore has no value.
- **Regulation reaches the chokepoints, not the protocol.** Capturing exchanges and custodian wallet providers is sufficient to sideline anonymity-focused coins.
- **Custodian wallet providers include key-safeguarding services.** Threshold, backup and recovery services fall inside the definition — most applications, hosted wallets and exchanges.
- **Jurisdiction shopping does not save anonymous coins.** Exchange services between virtual currencies are covered; simply not being located in a given state will not protect a system designed for illicit use.
- **Extraterritorial enforcement is already proven.** A non-FATF host state still cooperated to shut down Liberty Reserve; offshore incorporation is not a shield.
- **Privacy-with-traceability is the intended property.** Anonymity is a design flaw that guarantees being sidelined; lawful privacy is the property that keeps Bitcoin exchangeable.
