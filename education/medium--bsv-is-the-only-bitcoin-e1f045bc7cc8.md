# BSV is the only Bitcoin. — core principles

- **A system's identity is its rule-set.** "You do not get to change a system and say it is the same" — altering the protocol rules produces a different system, whatever name, ticker or brand it carries.
- **Protocol continuity, not market consensus, defines the platform.** System identity is not conferred by market capitalisation, hash-rate majority, exchange listings or brand recognition — only by continuity with the original protocol definition.
- **Builder's guarantee: a fixed protocol.** The platform property worth investing against is a locked, unchanging rule-set; a network whose rules can be redefined is a different platform after every change, and products built on it carry rule-change risk.
