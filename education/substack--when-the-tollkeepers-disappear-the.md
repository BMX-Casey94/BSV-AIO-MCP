# When the Tollkeepers Disappear — core principles

- **Most financial infrastructure exists to insert itself between value creators.** A 3–4% fee compounded across a chain of exchanges can siphon off twenty cents on the dollar through structural positioning rather than risk-bearing enterprise — "this is not an accident; it is a design."
- **Intermediary necessity is manufactured, not intrinsic.** "The system does not function because intermediaries are necessary; rather, intermediaries are necessary because the system has been constructed to require them" — and "it became complex because it required intermediaries", not the reverse.
- **Real digital cash has six defining properties.** Direct transferability without third-party permission; instant settlement final within seconds; micropayment capability at fractions of a cent; global reach; scalability to billions of transactions per day; and a deterministic, minimal fee structure — the checklist builders should be held to.
- **Final settlement collapses the intermediary layer's three functions.** Transaction processing becomes trivial (no clearinghouse, reconciliation or delay); risk management — largely a function of delayed settlement and counterparty exposure — mostly evaporates; information coordination becomes inherent in a unified ledger.
- **Fee arithmetic decides which business models exist.** Ten transactions per economic chain at 3% compound to ~26% value erosion; at fees of thousandths of a cent the erosion approaches zero, and a $0.01 payment bearing a 3% fee plus fixed cost is "effectively impossible" while $0.00003 fees make pay-per-use, granular content monetisation and machine-to-machine payment operational.
- **Settlement delay is a cost factory.** "Settlement delays introduce uncertainty. Uncertainty introduces risk. Risk introduces cost" — intermediaries monetise acceleration, guarantees and liquidity during delays they themselves impose; instant settlement reduces credit-line needs, capital requirements and settlement risk.
- **Remittance cost is a living-standards issue.** Workers losing 5–10% to fees and spreads suffer a material reduction in effective income; instant, transparent, negligible-fee transfer restores it directly to recipients.
- **Finality returns control to the individual.** Where accounts can be frozen, transactions reversed and access restricted, authority has been transferred away from the user; digital cash reverses this — "transactions, once made, are final; access is not contingent on approval" — a functional property, not an ideological one.
- **Throughput limits are design choices, not physics.** "Scalability is an engineering problem, not a conceptual one"; the efficiency gains from removing tollkeepers are redistributed to participants — disproportionately to frequent transactors, low-margin operators and the previously excluded.
