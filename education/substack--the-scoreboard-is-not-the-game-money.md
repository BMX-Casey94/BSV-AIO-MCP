# The Scoreboard Is Not the Game — core principles

- **Money is the calculative institution, not the wealth it measures.** A farm, factory or trained surgeon is wealth; a monetary balance is only a claim upon production. Printing claims upon bread does not bake bread.
- **Money emerges through exchange as the most saleable good.** It is an emergent solution to indirect exchange (Menger), not a legislative gift or a metaphysical substance called value. The unit of account is judged by purchasing power, not nominal figures.
- **An unstable unit is epistemic vandalism.** Inflation degrades economic language: it introduces noise into every contract, wage, pension and investment — like engineers building aircraft with rulers whose length changes by parliamentary debate.
- **Prices transmit dispersed knowledge.** Corrupting money corrupts the language of prices (Hayek). Monetary competition disciplines issuers because users can abandon inferior currencies.
- **A protocol whose rules developers can change is governed money.** Mutability converts money from law into policy and from measurement into power. Replication of the ledger is not autonomy.
- **Possession of keys without stable rules is tenancy.** Control over the rules defining what a signed transaction means matters more than key possession. "Not your keys, not your coins" is incomplete.
- **Maintenance is not revision.** A bridge can be maintained without redefining the metre; a protocol can be maintained without granting implementers authority over the monetary constitution. Sound money must be boring to the ambitious.
- **Digital cash must be cash.** Nakamoto's peer-to-peer electronic cash identifies money as a transactional system, not a hoarded token. Spectator copies of the rulebook do not decentralise a system whose rulebook can be revised mid-game.
- **Four tests for sound money.** Mengerian (emergence through exchangeability), Misesian (supports calculation), Hayekian (transmits dispersed knowledge), and constitutional (rules stable enough that users need not price insiders' ambitions).
