# Repudiation — core principles

- **Technical non-repudiation does not exist.** No cryptographic construction can settle the matter: a party can always claim lost keys, duress or another excuse.
- **A signature is evidence, not conclusive consent.** Key possession does not legally prove ownership or that the spender acted freely.
- **Fungibility is a legal property, not a mixing feature.** Bitcoin transactions are not made fungible by technical non-repudiation; they are fungible through law.
- **Fungibility arises from lawful exchange.** It comes when money is exchanged as consideration, without knowledge of crime, for valid consideration.
- **A gift is not fungible.** Gratuitous transfers lack the consideration that confers fungible status; tainted-coin analysis remains legally meaningful.
