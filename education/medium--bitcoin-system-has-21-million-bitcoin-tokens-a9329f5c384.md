# BitCoin system has 21 million BitCoin tokens. — core principles

- **The base unit is the satoshi.** Each bitcoin token has 10⁸ decimal places; the 10⁻⁸ slot is named a satoshi, and that is the smallest possible unit in the system.
- **One bitcoin is a shorthand.** 100 million satoshis equal 1 bitcoin — as dollars stand to cents, but with eight places rather than two.
- **Supply is a fixed-width integer field.** Bitcoin has sixteen possible integer places: eight in front of the decimal (the 21 million cap) and eight to the right (down to one satoshi).
- **Issuance was defined at creation.** The supply is a fixed issuance, not an ongoing emission that can be redesigned later.
- **Divisibility is for volume.** Treating the satoshi as the indivisible atom frames the money as roughly 2.1 quadrillion tokens, built for high-volume micropayments rather than coarse hoarding.
