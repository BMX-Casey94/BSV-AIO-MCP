# Bitcoin is not Anti State — it is Pro Honest government — core principles

- **Private is not anonymous.** Bitcoin is an open, immutable ledger; it is not anonymous, it is private — and money only works when it is not anonymous.
- **Privacy requires traceability.** The ability to access and validate a transaction is what makes it private in the meaningful sense; Bitcoin permits investigation, but only at a cost and with a proper audit trail.
- **Fungibility depends on records.** To be fungible, money needs to be linked to records — even cash is, through invoices and audit trails.
- **Auditability applies symmetrically.** The traceability rule binds users, corporations and government alike, not just the "community".
- **On-chain publication creates an accountable press.** Data written permanently to the ledger (e.g. Memo.cash) enables a free press — but a free press is accountable and therefore cannot be anonymous; anonymous trolling is a means to censor others by drowning them out.
- **Defamation is force, not speech.** Using falsehoods to attack and silence others is an attack, and those who censor with lies must be held accountable.
- **Bitcoin simplifies fair taxation.** A consumption tax (VAT/GST) can be applied equally at the point of spend while investment capital is left untouched; merchants can remit tax instantly with no forms, no intrusive audits and no large enforcement bureaucracy.
- **Anonymity favours the powerful.** Anonymous systems only favour the rich, the powerful and government; private-but-traceable money brings transparency, lets honest governments compete fairly, and curtails corruption and fraud.
- **Design intent: freedom through accountability.** Bitcoin is not anarchist; it is about freedom.
