## Problem addressed
Computational task-distribution services let a node outsource an expensive algorithm, but the requester cannot tell whether the reported result is correct without re-running the work. Scripting languages rich enough to adjudicate proposer-versus-challenger disputes (the specification cites Ethereum/Truebit) can host unpredictable programs. The document wants a marketplace that keeps funds on a restricted-script proof-of-work chain such as Bitcoin.

## Core mechanism
A requester posts a task and a bounty. A proposer commits to a hash of a solution and locks a deposit. If nobody challenges within a window, the proposer is paid on the main chain. If a challenger posts a competing string and deposit, members of an open-membership congress (nodes that have staked on the proof-of-work chain and hold threshold key shares in TEEs) deploy a temporary proof-of-stake “ghost chain”. Arbitration compares intermediate results from proposer and challenger, re-runs the step where they diverge on the ghost chain, then constructs a final transaction that distributes bounty and deposits under the congress threshold signature and records the outcome back to the main chain. The ghost chain then terminates; deposits stay locked while it runs to reduce the nothing-at-stake problem.

## Independent claims (gist — not legal scope)
Claim 1 is a method of monitoring a congress-managed computational task-distribution system for a challenge to a proposer’s hashed solution string (tied to a requester bounty and a proposer deposit), then resolving the challenge on a first blockchain by selecting among submitted solutions and distributing the digital assets under a congress threshold-signature scheme. Claims 12 and 13 are a system and a non-transitory medium that perform the method.

## BSV relevance
The specification is explicitly about hosting a computation marketplace on a Bitcoin-like restricted script by adding a congress and a temporary side chain. That bears on BSV designs that outsource verification or computation while keeping value on the main chain.
