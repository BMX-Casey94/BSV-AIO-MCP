# Why code must not be law — core principles

- **Agreements and courts govern, not code.** It is the agreement that binds, not the paper or the script; hearsay rules and documentary discrepancies are how truth is tested, not “proof of social media”.
- **Bitcoin works because you cannot erase the logs.** That undeletable trail is why the Byzantine problem is solvable here, and why anonymity layers and Lightning try to remove the property that makes the system strong.
- **The system is private, not anonymous.** An immutable audit trail is what resists operator-less crime; mixers plus conditionally provable keys are how you build a hydra with no head.
- **Possession is not ownership.** Holding a file, a key or a leaked document does not create title, and it does not prove authorship.
- **Code-is-law thinking licenses theft.** Treating software as self-executing justice is the mentality that treats insider IP grabs as fair game; injunctions and evidence are what stop them.
- **Bitcoin was designed so crime-friendly e-cash would fail.** Systems that enable autonomous ransomware or operator-less payouts are everything Bitcoin is not.
- **Metanet stores documents so they cannot be altered.** Putting the record on an immutable ledger is how you take alteration out of the hands of the people who would rewrite it.
