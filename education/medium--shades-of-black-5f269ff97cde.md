# Shades of Black… — core principles

- **Governments can stop a cryptocurrency.** The belief that no state can touch a coin is a delusion.
- **Bitcoin works by staying inside the law.** It survives because it lets most nations set policy around it, not because it evades them.
- **Transparency is a kleptocrat’s nightmare.** Corrupt governments are not helped by an honest ledger; they are exposed by it.
- **The benefit is conditional on the rule of law.** Where there is accountability, open government, impartial courts and stable just laws, Bitcoin can help promote a better society.
- **Legality is a spectrum, not a binary.** “Shades of black” is the right frame: Bitcoin is hostile to lawlessness and useful where law already holds.
