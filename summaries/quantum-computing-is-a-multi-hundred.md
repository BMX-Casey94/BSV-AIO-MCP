# Quantum Computing Is a Multi-Hundred-Billion-Dollar Fraud
**Date:** 2026-04-06 | **URL:** https://singulargrit.substack.com/p/quantum-computing-is-a-multi-hundred
**Subtitle:** No logical qubit has ever been built.

## Core thesis
No logical qubit — an encoded qubit that outperforms the physical qubits it is built from — has ever been constructed, on any platform, in thirty years. Every application claim of quantum computing collapses without this foundational unit, yet hundreds of billions of dollars in private and government investment have been raised on the premise that it is near. The gap between honest papers and their public communication constitutes a sustained, structural fraud.

## Key arguments and claims
- A logical qubit must show lower error rates, longer coherence, active mid-circuit error correction, and improving performance as physical qubits are added to the encoding. Nobody has demonstrated this; the field's own researchers admit it privately while participating in press releases implying otherwise.
- The Zhang et al. paper in *Nature Nanotechnology* (23 March 2026), "Universal logical operations in a silicon quantum processor", used five phosphorus nuclear spins in isotopically purified silicon implementing the [[4,2,2]] error-**detection** code. Its own numbers damn the milestone framing: logical coherence ~208 microseconds versus physical ~523 microseconds (encoding made coherence *worse* by more than half); logical gate fidelities ~86% versus physical >95%; the T gate at 82.6% only after postselection.
- The paper states explicitly: "Since we cannot perform mid-circuit measurements, stabilizer parity projections are implemented via postprocessing at the end of circuits in a destructive manner." Detection plus postselection is not error correction. The chemistry demonstration required three layered classical error-mitigation techniques to match theory.
- A thirty-year sequence repeats the pattern of real experiment, honest caveated paper, fraudulent public communication: Shor's 1994 algorithm; 1998 NMR gates; 2001's seven-qubit factoring of 15; D-Wave's 2007 announcement and 2011 sale to Lockheed Martin for ~$10 million (a 2014 *Science* paper found no quantum speedup); Google's 2019 "supremacy" claim (IBM showed the task was classically doable in 2.5 days, not 10,000 years); Microsoft's 2023 topological-qubit paper retraction; Harvard/QuEra 2023; Google's 2025 distance-3 surface code — memory-only, 49 physical qubits per logical qubit, still orders of magnitude from useful computation.
- The fraud mechanism needs no individual liar: researcher stays quiet to protect funding; the university press office strips caveats; journalists reproduce releases on deadline; investors follow coverage; programme officers cite milestones to justify budgets. Diffusion of responsibility does not change the outcome.
- Capital documented: over $30 billion private investment in the decade to 2024; government programmes (US NQI, EU Quantum Flagship, UK, China) totalling $50–100 billion; PsiQuantum raised over $1 billion with no working device; IonQ and Rigetti public via SPACs at ~$2 billion valuations; IBM's million-qubit roadmap would not yield one useful logical qubit at current error rates.
- Fault tolerance requires simultaneous solution of multiple open problems: two-qubit gate errors below ~0.01% (best demonstrated ~0.1%), real-time classical syndrome decoding, millions of physical qubits, all integrated in one system. Solving four of five yields nothing.
- The One Test: six conditions (lower logical error, longer logical coherence, better logical gates, real-time mid-circuit correction, improvement with code distance, demonstrated in gates not just memory). Every result in thirty years fails at least one; most fail all.
