# Scenario 3: Lease Contract
**Date:** 2018-10-06 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/scenario-3-lease-contract-d0ee4cd3900e
**Subtitle:** In this situation, Bob takes out a lease contract with Eve for a fixed term of three years. The terms of the contract will specify a…

## Core thesis
Third in the series, moving from open-ended registration to time-bound agreements: a fixed-term, three-year lease between Bob and Eve, with no break clauses, modelled as an on-chain state machine. Payment mechanics are explicitly deferred to later posts. The body is a short setup paragraph whose substantive model is a state-machine diagram (image not captured in the plain-text body).

## Key arguments and claims
- A fixed-term lease — here, three years between Bob (lessee) and Eve (lessor) — can be represented as a blockchain state machine.
- The contract "has a fixed term with no break clauses": unlike Scenarios 1-2, this arrangement has a defined termination, and no early-exit paths exist in the model.
- The lease "will specify a number of payments", but payment handling is "detailed within later posts" and deliberately out of scope here.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Continues the taxonomic state-machine method: each instalment isolates one contractual dimension — here, fixed duration and the absence of break clauses — and holds the rest constant. Real contract law categories (term, break clause, payment schedule) are mapped onto machine states and transitions, treating legal drafting as a specification problem.
