# Rent seeking in economics and Crypto — core principles

- **Incentives, not truth, explain which ideas get funded.** Economists are rational rent-seekers: paymasters reward reports that support the payer and bury dissent — the same public-choice structure appears in Bitcoin discourse.
- **Savings are not a vice.** The claim that savings lower productivity is a fallacy; Bitcoin’s fixed supply is not a defect of “demand management”.
- **Developers without skin in the game are rent-seekers.** Social-media consensus (proof of social media) pays people to tell audiences the lies they wish to hear.
- **Proof-of-work is the honest signal.** Hash power, not social-media following, decides; PoW ends the fatal flaws of proof-of-social-media governance.
- **Skin in the game is the filter.** Those who compete for the block reward have something to lose; those who only talk do not.
