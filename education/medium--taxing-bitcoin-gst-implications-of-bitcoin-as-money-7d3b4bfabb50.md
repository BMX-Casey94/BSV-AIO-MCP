# Taxing Bitcoin — GST implications of Bitcoin as money — core principles

- **GST taxes a form of transaction, not consumption.** A taxable supply under s.9–5 requires supply for consideration, in furtherance of an enterprise, connected with Australia, by a registered entity (Sterling Guardian).
- **Mining is a business but makes no taxable supply.** Miners satisfy the TR 97/11 business indicators (heavy hardware investment, systematic operation at scale, profit intention) and should register above the AUD $75,000 threshold — yet per GSTR 2006/9 a supply needs an identifiable recipient, and "you cannot make a supply to the world at large".
- **Block rewards have no counterparty.** Miners validate transactions without access to the parties, so the reward is not consideration for any supply: "mining activities alone would not give rise to taxable supplies by the miners" — validation is a competitive commercial activity, not a service to an identifiable principal.
- **Holding bitcoin creates no supplies** and hence no GST consequences at all.
- **Spending bitcoin as money is not a taxable event for the payer.** Section 9–10(4) excludes supplies of money; the supplier's liability follows ordinary rules, so a café taking bitcoin makes an ordinary taxable supply.
- **Valuation uses the foreign-currency formula.** Section 9–85 requires consideration in Australian currency, convertible per GSTR 2001/2 using rates quoted by bitcoin exchanges or providers like OANDA and XE.
- **Exchange trades follow currency-dealing treatment.** Sales of bitcoin may be input taxed (foreign currency, reg 40–5.09(3) item 9), GST-free to non-residents (s.38–190), or outside scope; exchange-held coins are treated as held on the user's behalf, so trades run between users.
- **The non-money alternative is a reductio.** If bitcoin were not money, every payment would create two taxable supplies — payer and payee taxing each other — a result "inconsistent with equivalent transactions where real-world currency is used" and a recipe for non-compliance.
