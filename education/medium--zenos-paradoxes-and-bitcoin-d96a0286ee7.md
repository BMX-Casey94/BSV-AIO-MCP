# Zeno’s Paradoxes and Bitcoin — core principles

- **The subsidy is a readable geometric series.** Starting at 50 bitcoin per block and halving every four years lets anyone see, at a glance, roughly how many bitcoin remain.
- **The series sums toward a fixed cap.** The halvings form a geometric sequence that approaches about 21 million coins.
- **Integer limits end the subsidy.** Unlike Zeno’s infinite dichotomy, finite integer precision makes the subsidy disappear around 2140.
- **Supply is sized for use, not fetishised scarcity.** There is an adequate number of bitcoin — more than needed for all the uses that will ever be developed. Divisibility, not scarcity theatre, does the work.
- **The 50-coin start is a design parameter.** The initial block subsidy was chosen so the remaining supply stays approximately readable as the halvings proceed.
