# Consortium and Crowdsourced Model for Next-Generation Research and Patent Development
**Date:** 2025-10-30 | **URL:** https://singulargrit.substack.com/p/consortium-and-crowdsourced-model
**Subtitle:** Raising Funds for an Integrated AI, Blockchain, and Networking Research Programme Targeting Twenty New Patents Over the Initial Phase

## Core thesis
Wright announces a three-year research and intellectual-property programme building on a corpus of "over three thousand patents" already filed, structured to produce at least twenty new patent families per year (sixty or more in total) across five technical domains. Funding follows a dual path — a formal investment consortium plus a non-equity crowdsourcing initiative launching "within the next fortnight" — with every invention packaged as a legally defensible, academically published and commercially licensable asset.

## Key arguments and claims
- Foundation and target: the programme extends "over three thousand patents I have already filed across the domains of artificial intelligence, scalable networking, distributed computation, and blockchain-based digital cash systems", with an initial phase targeting "twenty or more new patent filings" progressing into the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) framework.
- Five research domains: (1) AI — adaptive control, inference optimisation, federated learning, neural-symbolic reasoning, explainable AI, causal drift detection, hardware-level explainability "audit capsules", privacy-preserving model distribution with cryptographic proofs of learning integrity; (2) blockchain — stateless verification, audit-ready block propagation, "policy-enforced mining systems capable of executing authenticated legal directives such as freezes, reversals, or compliance holds", deterministic oracles embedding proof-of-source metadata, and programmable covenants enabling "lawful reversibility of transactions"; (3) network scalability — deterministic coordination at planetary scale, congestion-controlled deterministic routing, coflow-aware scheduling, carbon-optimised path management, bandwidth–latency equilibrium; (4) overlay/CDN systems — zero-trust CDN architectures, multicast within QUIC, erasure-coded transport, predictive caching, proof-of-origin at the protocol layer; (5) AI × blockchain integration — trusted training-data ledgers, anomaly-driven consensus mechanisms, model-version anchoring, smart-contract orchestration for regulated automation.
- Each patent package comprises six artefacts: UKIPO priority filing followed by PCT application; prior-art searches and legal examination; a formal peer-review-standard research paper; a practitioner whitepaper; implementation guidelines (APIs, message formats, conformance tests); and a use-case compendium.
- Inventor anonymity by design: filings execute under "a controlled and neutral entity", with inventor identities suppressed for five years from each filing date, then restored "through prearranged reassignment".
- Filing cadence: months 1–4 deliver six to eight UK filings; months 5–8 a second tranche plus preliminary PCT conversions; months 9–12 complete the twenty; years 2–3 repeat to a cumulative minimum of sixty. Quality gates: Gate A (novelty and claim integrity), Gate B (pre-publication legal clearance), Gate C (PCT strategy harmonisation and jurisdiction selection).
- Funding structure: consortium investors retain rights in a defined percentage of patents with family-level rights by sector, quarterly reporting and renewal options; the crowdsourcing tier is explicitly non-equity — participants gain "defined access to publication materials, early insight into patent development, and structured updates" — because "This non-equity structure prevents dilution of proprietary rights while promoting inclusivity and transparency."
- Initial capital deployment: recovery of accrued research and filing costs; procurement of GPU/TPU compute clusters, FPGA-based cryptographic accelerators, network emulation systems (QUIC, Anycast, multicast), high-availability storage arrays (ledger data, UTXO sets, provenance manifests), and Hardware Security Modules with Trusted Execution Environments.
- Governance: quarterly portfolio reports, annual portfolio reviews, independent technical and legal advisory panels, and counsel-reviewed embargo management aligned to filing stages.
- Commercialisation: tiered licensing by field-of-use and territory, time-bound exclusive options reverting to non-exclusive frameworks, mandatory conformance criteria for integrated AI × blockchain systems, and clauses preserving continuation and divisional rights.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
The post applies industrial-process logic to invention: research is organised as a repeatable, auditable production line in which every unit of output is simultaneously a legal instrument, an academic publication and an engineering specification. The commercial thesis is that verifiability and lawful auditability — deterministic, provably compliant systems — are the defensible differentiators at the intersection of AI, blockchain and networking, and that controlling the foundational patents there anchors future infrastructure in finance, data management and AI governance.
