# Satoshi; or, The Solution to Nakamoto’s Dilemma — core principles

- **An append-only record defeats myth-making.** Language and history are unstable and rewritten by the ambitious; a system that cannot change without recording the change always leads to the truth.
- **Miners enforce rules; they do not create them.** The white paper is clear that miners enforce the rules — an enforcer is not a creator.
- **Protocol meaning is anchored to the original text.** Later overlays that claim miners could make rules, or that Bitcoin is a system without government, are myths projected onto the white paper, not the text itself.
- **Predicates can be formed so that rhetorical dishonesty fails.** The immutable ledger is the technological answer to the instability of language and of rewritten history.
