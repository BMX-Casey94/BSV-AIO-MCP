# Ghosts of Gold — core principles

- **Real settlement turns keys, solves hashes and mines blocks.** Internal ledger entries, omnibus accounts and paper claims are fictional, conditional and revocable at will. Ownership that never withdraws is not ownership.
- **A trickle of actual coins cannot back a tide of synthetic claims.** Fractional-reserve float — a few per cent of real units supporting a superstructure of spreadsheet balances — manufactures an illusion of scarcity by limiting withdrawals, not by limiting claims.
- **Rehypothecation is the same asset on multiple books.** Inter-venue loans, dark pools and silent reuse of deposits recreate unregulated reserve banking without deposit insurance, capital accords or legal recourse.
- **Volume without velocity is theatre.** Bots and market makers trading with themselves generate numbers that dwarf real economies while cold wallets stay motionless. No keys turned, no hashes solved, no blocks mined.
- **Finality cannot be bounced.** Off-chain placeholder value that is routed rather than transferred makes settlement theoretical. Genuine digital cash does not tolerate float, netting or reversal: what you pay, stays paid, on-chain.
- **Scarcity is a property of the ledger, not of managed access.** KYC hurdles, withdrawal limits and delays are containment protocols. In a marketplace whose dominant incentive is never to withdraw, the first genuine redemption is the test.
