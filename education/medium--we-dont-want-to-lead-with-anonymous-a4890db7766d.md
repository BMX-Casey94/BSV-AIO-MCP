# We don’t want to lead with “anonymous” — core principles

- **Privacy is not anonymity.** Bitcoin leaves an audit trail: honest people get a private system that will never be anonymous.
- **Bitcoin was designed to work on the fine edge of law.** It was never meant to sit outside government or regulation.
- **No historical moment put Bitcoin beyond the state.** It can still be shut down; data centres can be stopped, and mere possession can be made illegal.
- **Exchanges are the control vector.** Unregulated “bucket shops” will be brought under the same rules as other money-service businesses.
- **AML cannot be designed away on Bitcoin.** There will never be a Bitcoin-based system that successfully gets around anti-money-laundering law.
- **Majority adoption is not won by opposing government.** For every anarchist there are at least a hundred people who do not want anarchy; a money that taunts the governments they vote for cannot become theirs.
- **Word choice is load-bearing.** Calling the system “anonymous” when the property is privacy invites the wrong users and the wrong law.
