# Satoshi Nakamoto — core principles

- **Code is not law.** Code is inflexible and has no heart or soul; the strength of the law is that it can be flexible. Those who treat code as law abrogate their responsibility to the society they live in.
- **Change law through democracy, not by discarding it.** In a democracy you work to change the law; anarchy is a utopian path to destruction and totalitarian control.
- **Privacy and anonymity are polar opposites.** Anonymity in its truest form is the heart of crime and deception; those who equate it with privacy are drawing people into the shadows.
- **Concealment is the beginning of lying and stealing.** The Tokugawa merchant-philosopher Tominaga Nakamoto's teaching is the philosophical key: secrecy as habit is the opposite of a lawful monetary system.
- **Bitcoin destroys anonymity and allows privacy.** It does so with an immutable evidence trail — privacy without untraceability.
- **Bitcoin was designed to scale beyond the finance casino.** The real market is the exchange of goods between individuals for consumption, not speculative exchange.
- **Lock the protocol and remove asinine changes.** Once the protocol is fixed and set in stone, it remains set in stone.
- **Risk cannot be perfected away.** Risk is a probabilistic ideal; those who refuse to understand that have no idea of people or society.
- **Fraud recycles under a freedom banner.** Dematerialised penny-share and ICO schemes pump false gains while removing the controls that protect the average person.
