# Death and taxes, it is time to kill off mythical beasts — core principles

- **Bitcoin makes tax easier to collect, not harder.** The two simplest taxes — consumption tax and income tax — survive intact; VAT/GST "can be collected on sale and exchange", and Bitcoin "simplifies corporate compliance and tax reporting allowing governments to collect faster and with less interference".
- **The January 2009 protocol already suffices for automated tax.** "Bitcoin as it existed in January 2009 allowed this to occur. In fact, there is no way to stop this" — no protocol changes are required for point-of-sale smart-contract tax collection.
- **VAT is the simplest digital-economy tax.** Bitcoin-linked accounting gives pseudonymous interaction that preserves corporate privacy while letting an organisation submit the correct amount; today's VAT complexity comes from input/output credit differentiation, which automation removes.
- **Smart contracts automate the whole compliance chain.** Each layer of processing can be individually accounted with automated payments based on existing tax laws lodged in software "that report instantly and not only report but transact instantaneously with government" — differential taxation (e.g. bakery versus tinned goods) can be encoded so the correct tax transfers to government at the moment of sale.
- **Government wallet hierarchies replace paper records.** A registered taxpayer transmits VAT directly from point-of-sale software to a hierarchical set of government wallets determined in advance; invoices attach to transactions for instantaneous reporting and refunds, eliminating years of messy paper records.
- **Consumption tax favours investment over consumption.** The idle wealthy "are forced to either invest or to pay down what they have in taxes".
- **Income tax persists through withholding incentives.** Employers remit employee tax because otherwise they do not obtain a deduction — it is not in the employer's interest to pay under the table; private payroll in bitcoin is "extremely simple" once wallets are designed properly.
- **Corporate tax is near-impossible to evade on-chain.** Firms must keep financial records regardless, and inter-company payments give government a complete, up-to-date record of transactions nearly instantaneously — a translucent audit layer preserving lower-level privacy.
- **The remedy for bad policy is political, not technical.** "Bitcoin does not remove government, that is something people in a democratic society vote on"; Bitcoin is sound money that "does nothing to circumvent the law. It takes no power away from government."
- **Privacy coins are structurally fragile.** Government can control access to payments and exchange and require that transactions in media it dislikes become immediately void — "the idea that privacy coins as they called will ever take traction is asinine".
- **Tax evasion is fraud against honest people.** "Evading tax is not noble, it is simply cheating and fraud… it means those who cannot, who will not and who are honest are punished for your transgression."
