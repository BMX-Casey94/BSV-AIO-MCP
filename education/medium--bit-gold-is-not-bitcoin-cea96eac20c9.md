# Bit Gold Is Not BitCoin — core principles

- **Bit gold and Bitcoin share a name, not an architecture.** Bit gold has every user mint their own proof-of-work money, registered and timestamped, with no blockchain and no supply limit — in many ways the opposite of Bitcoin.
- **Bitcoin has no challenge string.** Bit gold starts with a challenge string; Bitcoin does not.
- **Individuals do not run nodes in Bitcoin.** Miners validate transfers between individuals; users do not each create and register their own proof-of-work.
- **A blockchain (timechain) is essential.** There is no blockchain or timechain in bit gold, and nothing related to it.
- **Bitcoin has a supply limit; bit gold does not.** A system that lets all users individually create their own money is not Bitcoin.
- **Stamped coinage was about control, not trust.** Trusted third parties stamped and assayed coin so they could control the system, not so that it could be trusted.
- **Even stamped gold was still weighed.** Clipping, bag-wear and ordinary use reduce the metal in a coin, so where gold was used it was still weighed.
- **Working proof-of-work predates the slogan.** Proof-of-work dates to the 1990s; a replaceable proof-of-work system that actually functioned and was well coded already existed (Finney's RPOW).
- **Bitcoin is not a cypherpunk anarchist system.** It is designed against that lineage, not as its fulfilment.
