# PII in the Bitcoin World — core principles

- **Ask why before protecting data.** The most commonly missed issue in security is why: investigate the cause and need behind protecting personally identifiable information before designing defences.
- **PII protection is fraud prevention, not secrecy.** The point is to stop unauthorised applications and changes to a credit file — credit cards and home loans in someone else’s name — not to treat raw data secrecy as an end in itself.
- **Obscurity is not a security control.** Hiding one’s head in the sand and leaving trust to obscurity is not a strategy; it deserves no sympathy when it fails.
- **The risk is transactional, not informational.** The issue is not whether a criminal can buy your information cheaply, but whether they can steal money from you.
- **Cheap data markets do not decide the threat.** That PII “would sell for cents in the dollar” is irrelevant if the fraud it might enable is what must actually be stopped.
- **Security is process and responsibility.** Design around whether the harm is executable, not around panic that data exists on a market.
