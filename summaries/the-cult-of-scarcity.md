# The Cult of Scarcity
**Date:** 2025-12-03 | **URL:** https://singulargrit.substack.com/p/the-cult-of-scarcity
**Subtitle:** From Euphoric Mirage to Ledger-State

## Core thesis
BTC is not merely an economic error but a political and cultural pathogen. Structurally unable to function as cash, it survives only as a price cult; price cults require perpetual converts, narrative policing and resource parasitism; and their long-run equilibrium is an Orwellian "ledger-state" of rationed access, surveillance custodians, hereditary rentiers and permanent manufactured conflict.

## Key arguments and claims
- Wealth is productive capacity, not price: "Price is an afterimage. Treating it as substance is the first small treason against reality."
- The throughput ceiling is fate: at five transactions per second a system must shrink to a boutique settlement toy, migrate into custodianship, or ration by price; "power consolidates where capacity is, and capacity is scarce by design."
- The euphoric dawn was functional, not accidental: a non-productive asset with no yield "must recruit converts to keep the price rising for those who arrived first"; prophecy "is mechanics."
- BTC is "a price religion with a settlement altar": the chart is scripture, HODL is a vow, and critics "are not answered; they are anathematised."
- Wealth transfer is the engine: no dividend, no surplus, only the next buyer — "the 'greater fool' logic in polite dress"; evangelism is structural, not optional.
- The energy sink: mining "consumes productive inputs to maintain an inert scarcity token"; the loop (price up → mining up → power diverted → scarcity → token price up) is "economic cannibalism," producing rationing and a second aristocracy of energy barons.
- Capital misallocation sanctified: hoarding becomes an ethic, builders are mocked as naïve, venture capital thins, and "stores that never re-enter circulation become tombs, not bridges."
- The war function: the system needs enemies — "fiat tyrants," "nocoiners," "environmental saboteurs," "protocol traitors" — because "peace invites accounting. And accounting is the one thing this religion cannot endure."
- Ledger-state governance: custodians return by logistical necessity, then govern; KYC, blacklists and "clean coin" doctrines become routine; "The Orwellian aspect does not arrive with jackboots; it arrives with 'terms of service.'"
- Hyper-inequality becomes hereditary: "the top 0.1% of the 0.1%" live off rent from possession rather than production, and inheritance passes down "the right to extract," not competence.
- The system depends on load-bearing lies about money (redefined as "store of value"), throughput ("bottlenecks are renamed 'security'"), energy (defended mystically), distribution (concentration sanctified as "vision" and "merit") and history (retconned into inevitability).
- Production collapses into ritual: conferences multiply "like fungus," scapegoats are rolled out on schedule, and "zeal is the social technology that keeps poverty obedient."
- The alternative: real digital cash competition means on-chain scale, fees falling as adoption rises, miners competing "to include more transactions, not fewer," and capital rewarded for building — progress "by allowing reality to choose."

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Political economy and incentive analysis pushed to systemic conclusion, framed by Orwell's model of managed scarcity and permanent war. Each section traces a mechanism (bottleneck, transfer, energy burn, hoarding) into the institution it must birth (custodians, surveillance, aristocracy, propaganda), treating dystopia as the downstream of incentives rather than of malevolence.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- The store-of-value redefinition of money is "the foundational lie": money is a high-volume instrument of trade.
- The fee market is rationing by price, not a healthy market signal; congested mempools are failure, not worthiness.
- HODL ethics freeze capital into reverence; building, not holding, is what creates wealth.
- Energy burn is parasitism with mystical cover, not "security" in any economically defensible sense.
- "Decentralisation" claims mask custodial re-intermediation; Lightning-era finance is banking with better branding.
- Early-holder concentration is extraction by timing, not merit or vision.
