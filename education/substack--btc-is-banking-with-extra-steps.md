# BTC Is Banking with Extra Steps — core principles

- **Peer-to-peer means a triangle, not a relay of hops.** Alice pays Bob directly, IP-to-IP; either or both submit the transaction to a node that settles it in a block. Nodes are the competitive systems that build blocks, process, validate, order and settle — the settlement infrastructure, not hobby machines.
- **A throttled base layer manufactures the intermediary stack.** Restrict capacity, drive fees up and push commerce into secondary systems, and direct payment becomes a luxury ritual. The maze is the deviation from the design, not the design.
- **Architecture determines economics, economics incentives, incentives behaviour.** A system that drives users through intermediaries becomes an account system — permissioned routing, liquidity policy, custodial risk and fee pressure. It becomes what it claimed to replace.
- **Cash must work at ordinary scale.** Small, frequent and commercial payments, merchants, automated services, micropayments, machine-to-machine exchange — boringly functional. Cash cannot be an occasional ceremony.
- **Lightning postpones the constraint; it does not escape it.** Opening and closing channels, resolving disputes and final settlement still touch the throttled chain. A routed channel network is a different architecture from electronic cash, not a layer on top of it.
- **Security is funded by volume at low cost, not by punitive tolls.** As subsidy declines, nodes are paid through fees that can arise from many cheap transactions. Many transactions at low cost are economically superior to a small number of expensive ones if the goal is a functioning commercial system.
- **Users need to transact, not to become settlement infrastructure.** A node is a competitive block-building business. The word Bitcoin belongs to the system of peer-to-peer electronic cash.
