# The Great Global Skim — core principles

- **Intermediation that produces only movement is a skim.** Formal finance plus shadow banking — money-market funds, SIVs, conduits, repo and rehypothecation that make a single pound appear on multiple balance sheets — extract tens of trillions a year for a caste that produces nothing but circulation.
- **Opacity is the business model.** Complexity is the sanctuary in which responsibility is buried; a shadow-bank that prevented crises would forfeit its raison d'être. Confuse the public, then charge them for the clarification.
- **Wealth is production, not the price of a coin.** Labour and ingenuity transforming matter — the brick, the bridge, the crop, the machine — create value. Capital that spins without moving forward is a top that never falls only because it never advances.
- **Direct exchange is the remedy.** A handshake made cryptographically visible; a contract enforceable without a pilgrimage through institutional purgatory. Verification replaces trust and ends absurdities such as a ten-pound fee to move a pound, and correspondent-bank mazes.
- **Scarcity is a condition to overcome.** Through invention, labour and production — not a sacralised austerity. A cash system that cannot function at commercial throughput is not a medium of exchange.
- **Capital should flow toward creation, not circulation.** Redirecting even a fraction of the intellect spent on siphoning micro-fractions of value would redraw the boundary of the possible: infrastructure, poverty reduction and the next industrial renaissance rather than a priesthood of exchanges and custodians.
