# The Economy Has Always Been Data — core principles

- **What it costs to measure, attest, verify and enforce has always bounded exchange.** The economy has always been data-driven. "Knowledge economy" is a rediscovery of Rand, Hayek and North, not a new dispensation.
- **The economic problem is dispersed knowledge, not allocative logic.** Knowledge is never given to a single mind (Hayek 1945). The price system compresses scarcity signals into actionable form. Bigger computers do not rescue central planning: local knowledge loses its time-value in collection.
- **Transaction costs are the costs of measuring attributes and enforcing agreements.** North, building on Coase: the costliness of information is the key to the costs of transacting. Western divergence is the cumulative invention of cheaper transacting — bills of exchange, negotiability, Champagne fairs, price currents, marine insurance.
- **A tool is any instrument that lowers the per-unit cost of an economic operation.** Tally sticks, coinage, double-entry bookkeeping, the telegraph, the standard container, the relational database, SWIFT, ACH. None invented a knowledge economy; each extended one that already existed.
- **Incumbent rails are locked in, not optimal.** A successor is evaluated on whether it reduces unit cost, by how much, on what attributes, and at what credibility (David; Arthur).
- **Bitcoin is cheap, atomic, verifiable settlement — the next tool, not a discontinuity.** Transfer against a tamper-evident public record. Independent verification reported zero double-spend violations across approximately 520 million unspent outputs and 12.8 million deliberate attempts; validated throughput of 1.12 million transactions per second per node.
- **Atomic settlement eliminates artefacts, it does not compete with them.** Reconciliation between sub-ledgers, settlement-finality delay, chargeback windows and correspondent routing are products of pre-atomic architectures. The bill of exchange did not make the coin-caravan faster; it made the caravan unnecessary for that operation.
- **There is no metaphysical novelty.** Cheaper verifiable settlement is exactly what the observation predicts. There is only the next tool.
