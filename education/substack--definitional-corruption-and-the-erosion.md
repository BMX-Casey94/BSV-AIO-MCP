# Definitional Corruption and the Erosion of Truth: A Wittgensteinian Analysis of BTC Debates — core principles

- **Meaning is rule-governed use, and rule-following is communal.** Wittgenstein's later philosophy holds that words have no inner essence; the builder-and-assistant parable ("brick", "slab") shows words functioning as moves in a rule-governed practice, and claiming to follow a rule while acting contrary to the shared practice is unintelligible.
- **The 2008 white paper constituted a coherent language game.** "Transaction", "block", "chain" and "proof-of-work" were anchored in concrete processes, so "Is this Bitcoin?" could be answered by checking a system against defined rules — truth by practice, not rhetoric. That fixed semantic anchor is what makes a protocol evaluable at all.
- **Redefinition while keeping the name is misappropriation, not evolution.** Substituting "store of value" for electronic cash, fixing an artificial block-size limit and retaining familiar word-shells while transforming their use severs words from the rules that gave them meaning — "not evolution, not innovation, but substitution".
- **A "layer" must extend the base rules.** Lightning "is not layered atop Bitcoin in the sense of extending its rules but is an external system of payment channels that bypasses the blockchain entirely"; calling it "Layer 2" while its use no longer corresponds to the original rules is "a kind of conceptual fraud" — a technical distinction builders must hold when evaluating settlement claims.
- **Institutional repetition confers no legitimacy against the rules.** Exchanges entrenching a meaning by ticker listing ("whatever occupied it was Bitcoin by fiat of institutional control"), media amplification and academic citation networks manufacture apparent consensus — but "Fidelity to rules must outweigh popularity."
- **Definitional capture destroys adjudicability.** When two incompatible language games claim one name, "transaction", "scaling" and "blockchain" become floating signifiers; debate becomes irresolvable because truth requires shared criteria of correctness. "Whoever controls the definitions controls the discourse."
- **Builder guidance.** Demand definitional fidelity before investing in a network: fidelity to the white paper's original text, communal reinforcement by enacting the original design (building, transacting, demonstrating on-chain scaling), vigilance that names each distortion without euphemism, and honesty in use — a network whose terms can be redefined under you offers no stable platform.
