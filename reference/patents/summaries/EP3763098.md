## Problem addressed
Electronic submissions that must stay sealed until every participant has filed (tenders, offers, examination papers) normally depend on a manager’s honesty and on the security of a central store. The specification says submitted files can be read or altered too early, and that paper-style sealed envelopes do not translate to computer networks.

## Core mechanism
Participating nodes hold shares of a collective key pair (p, P) and each also has a one-time nodal pair (q_i, Q_i). A document is encrypted under L_i = P + Q_i and stored in a public repository; a hash of the document is recorded on chain. Each participant opens a commitment channel with a principal: a commitment transaction locks value so that a later reveal, rescind or timeout transaction can spend it. A reveal spend publishes the node’s key share, its nodal private key and the document hash; a rescind spend publishes only the share (withdrawing the document from consideration but still contributing to the collective key); a timeout after deadline s1 forfeits the deposit to the principal. Once enough shares appear on chain, any node reconstructs p, forms l_i = p + q_i, decrypts, and checks the hash. Documents therefore become readable together and are shown to be unaltered.

## Independent claims (gist — not legal scope)
Claim 1 is a method, at a first node, of verifying a digital resource: form a crypto public key from the node’s public key plus a collective public key; encrypt the resource; build a commitment channel by creating a commitment transaction whose output is locked by the node’s private-key share and a subsequent transaction that spends it and includes that share, then receiving the subsequent transaction signed by a principal node; and broadcasting the commitment transaction. Claims 14 and 15 are a computing device and a processor-readable medium that carry out the method.

## BSV relevance
The embodiment uses Bitcoin-style commitment/refund channels, on-chain publication of key shares and hashes, and threshold reconstruction so sealed submissions open together. That is relevant to BSV applications that need coordinated reveal of off-chain documents.
