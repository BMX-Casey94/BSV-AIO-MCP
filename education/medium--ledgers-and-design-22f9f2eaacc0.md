# Ledgers and Design — core principles

- **The blockchain is a WORM journal.** Double-entry bookkeeping has required write-once-read-many tables for over 800 years; Bitcoin restores that property in a digital ledger so alteration is self-evident.
- **Errors are corrected by new entries.** Mistakes are not deleted or reversed; a new visible entry corrects them, leaving the original for auditors.
- **UTXOs form parallel journals.** Grouping unspent outputs lets the chain carry private yet secure sub-ledgers by account, built on top of Bitcoin.
- **Miners maintain the ledger for a fee.** Honest nodes can append records; Bitcoin is clear text, so court-ordered reassignment of registry entries is technically possible.
- **Bitcoin prevents dishonest cheating, not lawful seizure.** Mixers taint everything they touch under proceeds-of-crime law; isolated UTXOs let honest recipients prove bona fide purchase.
- **The chain is a registry, not the right.** You own bitcoin only with good title and provenance, not mere possession. Each satoshi is an indivisible isolated token (100 million per coin).
- **Non-mining machines are not nodes.** Systems that do not mine are slow, useless sybils; they serve no purpose. Only miners are nodes.
- **Tax is inescapable and simpler on-chain.** Employer reporting with a fully auditable, traceable record facilitates tax without fraud or evasion.
- **Compliant miners out-earn boycotters.** Exchange seizure and freezing orders leave non-compliant miners earning a tiny fraction of those who follow court orders, so honest miners win.
- **Non-reversal is for small casual payments.** Mediation costs remain for large transfers; the white paper never made every payment absolutely irreversible.
