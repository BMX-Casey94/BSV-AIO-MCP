# I avoid bullshit and scams.
**Date:** 2018-09-18 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/i-avoid-bullshit-and-scams-20407b5f252e
**Subtitle:** There is Bitcoin, or there is a bunch of idiots who do not understand what Bitcoin is.

## Core thesis
A three-line polemic: there is exactly one Bitcoin, and everything else is either a scam or the product of people who do not understand what Bitcoin is. No argument is offered — the post is a purity declaration, delivered in the middle of the pre-split BCH hash-war build-up (September 2018), where "Bitcoin" in Craig's usage means Bitcoin Cash as the original protocol.

## Key arguments and claims
- "I avoid bullshit and scams." — a self-positioning statement: his projects and associations are confined to the one true Bitcoin.
- "There is Bitcoin, or there is a bunch of idiots who do not understand what Bitcoin is." — a binary taxonomy: the single legitimate chain versus a undifferentiated mass of scammers and the clueless.
- "The end." — the refusal to argue is itself the rhetorical move: the claim is presented as self-evident and closed to debate.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Pure assertion as rhetoric. Rather than the economic or historical argumentation of his longer essays, this post uses aphorism and insult to draw an in-group/out-group boundary. It functions less as persuasion than as a loyalty signal and a framing device: all subsequent technical disagreement (with BTC, and imminently with the ABC side of BCH) is pre-classified as idiocy or fraud.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Denies the legitimacy of the entire rest of the crypto ecosystem by fiat — BTC included by implication — collapsing the "many legitimate experiments" pluralism of mainstream crypto discourse into a single-chain binary.
- Notably, it does this without naming any chain: the polemic works by leaving "Bitcoin" undefined, letting his concurrent BCH advocacy fill in the referent.
