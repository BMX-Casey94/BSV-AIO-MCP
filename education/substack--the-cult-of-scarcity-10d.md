# The Cult of Scarcity (December 8) — core principles

- **Wealth is productive capacity.** The acre that feeds ten where it once fed one; creation is the mother of civilisation and transfer only its scavenger — a price rise with no underlying capacity is a reshuffling of claims, not the creation of wealth.
- **Money is a communications channel for value.** A narrow channel either prices out all but the rich or drives users away; a five-transactions-per-second ceiling is an economic verdict, not a technical detail.
- **Layer-2 and custodial shims are displacement, not scaling.** A monetary substrate that needs scaffolding to function has admitted, in the only language that matters, that it does not function; the external structure becomes the system in practice, reviving fiat-style intermediaries with greater leverage.
- **Greater-fool arithmetic is a counting argument.** A non-productive asset yields no cashflow, so earlier holders' profit is sourced entirely from later holders' expenditure — there is no third well from which value is drawn, only the queue; universal adoption fails by logic because no buyer base remains.
- **Energy expenditure carries opportunity cost.** Each watt burned is a watt not burned elsewhere; security spend should buy throughput, not scarcity — spending electricity to preserve a bottleneck and then selling the bottleneck as destiny is economic cannibalism.
- **Rewarding hoarding misallocates capital.** Every pound locked in sterile scarcity is a pound not at work in fertile enterprise; the damage is temporal — the unbuilt bridge, the unformed startup, the laboratory running on fumes.
- **High fees are a regressive toll.** Low throughput guarantees fees whose burden rises as one's purse shrinks; such a system is not a ladder for the many but a moat for the few.
- **The positive criteria for digital cash.** Base-layer on-chain scaling; low fees achieved by letting supply meet demand; competitive block creation as a service industry for commerce; circulation encouraged rather than withholding sanctified. Money is function first, faith as residue.
