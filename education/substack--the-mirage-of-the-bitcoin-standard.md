# The Mirage of the Bitcoin Standard — core principles

- **A monetary base that cannot settle the claims upon it forces paper.** The gold standard failed mechanically: physical settlement could not accompany industrial commerce, so bullion banks issued paper against fractional reserves — a single ounce underpinning twenty of paper, the equilibrium held only by confidence.
- **Throughput arithmetic makes mass redemption impossible.** Five TPS is 432,000 transactions per day, about 157 million per year for the planet. One hundred million users settling once a month would monopolise the ledger for months; a billion users implies years of continuous block space per monthly cycle.
- **Crisis closures cannot clear the pipe.** Ten million unilateral closures in a week against roughly 3 million transactions of weekly capacity forces most to wait while time-locks run and fees explode.
- **Custodial gateways are digital bullion banks.** Exchanges ration, delay and batch withdrawals while internal transfers clear instantly; managed channel wallets are indistinguishable from deposit-taking; wrapped coins are fractional reserve in exile.
- **One coin, three claims is structural, not rogue.** A deposit supports the depositor’s balance, a loan and the exchange’s operational liquidity; leveraged futures settle in internal credits. Credit multiplies precisely because settlement is scarce.
- **Fixed supply does not imply fixed discipline.** It implies the proliferation of abstractions: each coin can spawn twenty off-chain IOUs. The smaller the base, the more elaborate the derivatives it must spawn to survive.
- **The rupture is a suspension of convertibility.** A custodian fails, withdrawals rush, fees skyrocket, and “temporary suspension” ratifies what arithmetic already made true — Nixon’s gesture with pseudonyms.
- **Scale genuinely, or admit the digital-bullion regime.** High fees and congestion are the licensing conditions for leverage, not virtue signals. The gold standard functioned because most people never asked for their gold back.
