# Cryptographic Control Is Fiduciary Power, Not Title — core principles

- **Control over an asset is not ownership of it.** Cryptographic control is gatekeeping power over another person's proprietary position — not a new kind of title, but a new surface on which an old problem appears: power over another's property.
- **Map power, not labels.** The method is a power map: who can sign, delay, impose conditions, redirect, commingle, control recovery, and where precisely discretion sits. Terms of service saying "you remain the owner" while reserving rights to delay, freeze, substitute, rehypothecate, net internally and refuse withdrawals are marketing, not analysis.
- **Recognise the recurring gatekeeping architectures.** Single-key custody (customer dependent on an internal book entitlement); 2-of-3 multi-signature escrow (a built-in gatekeeper holding a veto); threshold/MPC custody (a committee decision concealed behind what looks on-chain like a single signature); smart contracts with admin keys (pause, upgrade, emergency withdrawal and oracle control as meta-control); exchange custody (omnibus wallets, internal ledgers, withdrawal queues, compliance holds).
- **Trust classification uses the orthodox three certainties.** Intention (objective — words and conduct, not labels), subject matter (identifiability supplied by records, reconciliation and defined pool boundaries — pooling does not defeat certainty), and constitution (placing the asset under the custodian's control architecture with acceptance of the undertaking).
- **Commingling cuts both ways.** It may evidence that segregation was never intended, or it may be a breach of a prior undertaking to segregate — a distinction that changes remedies and priorities, especially in insolvency.
- **The debt model is legitimate if candid.** What must not be tolerated is the hybrid: marketing custody while reserving free use and discretionary control — the comforting fiction of custody paired with the quiet reality of discretionary dominion.
- **Fiduciary duty is a loyalty constraint triggered by position.** Veto powers (delay, freeze, refuse, condition, release) are precisely the powers usable as leverage, preference or profit devices; equity demands no conflict without authorisation, no unauthorised profit, proper purposes, and even-handed administration where multiple parties are entitled.
- **Watch the standard conflict patterns.** Undisclosed use or rehypothecation; profit through delay (liquidity management, fee extraction); selective processing and internal preference in pooled systems; "compliance holds" used for liquidity, retaliation or extraction. Partial powers ("we cannot move anything unilaterally") can be the most dangerous because easiest to deny.
- **Equity supervises persons, not protocols.** The in personam jurisdiction asks whether discretion was exercised in good faith, for proper purposes, on relevant considerations, without caprice or disloyal self-interest; breadth of a power enlarges rather than reduces the need for proper-purpose policing. Remedies — injunctions, freezing orders, information orders, tracing into substitutes, account of profits, structural relief — respond to power without rewriting code.
