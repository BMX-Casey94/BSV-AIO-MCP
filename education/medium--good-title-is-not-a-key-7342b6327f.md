# Good Title Is Not a Key — core principles

- **Keys are not law.** A private key makes access to funds difficult; it does not prove ownership of property rights, and that was never Bitcoin’s intention.
- **Non-repudiation does not exist.** Law always admits error, intoxication, theft and duress, so a digital signature or a bitcoin transfer can be repudiated; chasing cryptographic non-repudiation is a pipe dream.
- **Bitcoin does not leave the real world.** There is a person at the end of every keyboard, and courts can order people to do acts.
- **Title, not possession, is ownership.** A stolen car key does not transfer the car; tracing and recovery restore the asset. Bitcoin is no different.
- **Bitcoin is recoverable.** Equitable tracing lets courts follow substituted assets; miners and exchanges, as corporations, can implement reciprocal agreements for international recovery.
- **Mining concentrates into lawful infrastructure.** Server-farm mining is the intended end state: capital-intensive corporate miners who defy a court order risk contempt and a multi-hundred-million-pound investment.
- **Lawful miners outcompete the rest.** A miner who processes a frozen transaction produces a block other miners will reject and loses money. Survival of the fittest miner is the one that stays within the law.
- **The system aligns with law over time.** Criminal-friendly miners are slowly and inevitably driven from the market as capital and jurisdiction bite.
