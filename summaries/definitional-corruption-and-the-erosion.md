# Definitional Corruption and the Erosion of Truth: A Wittgensteinian Analysis of BTC Debates
**Date:** 2025-09-25 | **URL:** https://singulargrit.substack.com/p/definitional-corruption-and-the-erosion
**Subtitle:** Language Games, Misappropriation, and the Collapse of Meaning in the Contest over Bitcoin

## Core thesis
The BTC controversy is at root a contest over language. Read through Wittgenstein's account of meaning as rule-governed use within language games, BTC Core's redefinition of Bitcoin from an electronic cash system into a speculative "store of value" was not terminological drift but deliberate definitional misappropriation. By severing words from the rules that gave them meaning while pretending continuity, BTC Core and its institutional allies destroyed the shared language game that made truth adjudicable — and defending Bitcoin's original definitions is therefore inseparable from defending intellectual integrity itself.

## Key arguments and claims
- Wittgenstein's later philosophy (Philosophical Investigations) holds that words have no inner essence; meaning is manifest in use. The builder-and-assistant parable ("brick", "slab") shows words functioning as moves in a rule-governed practice, and rule-following is communal: claiming to follow a rule while acting contrary to the shared practice is unintelligible.
- The 2008 white paper constituted a coherent language game: "transaction", "block", "chain" and "proof-of-work" were anchored in concrete processes, so the question "Is this Bitcoin?" could be answered by checking a system against defined rules — truth by practice, not rhetoric.
- The rupture: BTC Core substituted "store of value" for digital cash, fixed an artificial block-size limit, and branded Lightning a "Layer 2" — though Lightning "is not layered atop Bitcoin in the sense of extending its rules but is an external system of payment channels that bypasses the blockchain entirely". The word "layer" was retained while its use was transformed.
- Misappropriation became an instrument of power: exchanges entrenched the new meaning by listing the altered system under the BTC ticker ("whatever occupied it was Bitcoin by fiat of institutional control"); media amplified it; academics manufactured apparent consensus by citing one another while ignoring the white paper's cash functionality.
- The mechanism mirrors Orwell's political corruption of words: familiar shells ("freedom", "democracy", "Bitcoin") are kept while content is inverted, so opposition becomes unintelligible — appealing to the original definitions is dismissed as anachronistic.
- Consequence: two incompatible language games claim one name. "Transaction", "scaling" and eventually "blockchain" itself become floating signifiers; debate is irresolvable because truth requires shared criteria of correctness, and those criteria have been fractured.
- The stakes exceed Bitcoin: definitional capture is "a weapon of the age"; tolerating it surrenders philosophy to propaganda and corrodes trust, since knowingly misappropriating definitions is "a form of betrayal".
- Restoration requires four steps: fidelity to the white paper's original text; communal reinforcement by enacting the original game (building, transacting, demonstrating on-chain scaling); philosophical vigilance that names each distortion without euphemism; and an ethical commitment to honesty in use.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Applied philosophy of language: Wittgenstein's framework (language games, rule-following, family resemblance) is used as a diagnostic instrument, first establishing the white paper's semantic coherence, then tracing the genealogy of its corruption, then analysing how institutional repetition converts redefinition into hegemony. The argument is structural rather than empirical — it aims to show that the BTC debate's irresolvability is a logical consequence of corrupted meaning, not a clash of facts.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Denies that "digital gold"/"store of value" is a legitimate evolution of Bitcoin; it is a different game masquerading as the old one — "not evolution, not innovation, but substitution".
- Denies that Lightning is a "layer" of Bitcoin at all; calling it one is "a kind of conceptual fraud".
- Rejects the authority of repetition: exchange tickers, media narratives and academic citation networks manufacture consensus but confer no legitimacy against the white paper's rules — "Fidelity to rules must outweigh popularity."
- Reframes mainstream slogans ("safety", "decentralisation", "scaling") as corrupted terms whose altered grammar pre-judges technical debate.

## Notable quotes
- "The corruption of definition is the corruption of truth, and nowhere is this more evident than in the linguistic battlefield surrounding BTC."
- "To speak of 'Layer 2 scaling' when the architecture described no longer corresponds to the original rules is to engage in a kind of conceptual fraud."
- "Whoever controls the definitions controls the discourse, and whoever controls the discourse controls perception."
- "To defend Bitcoin as digital cash is thus to defend the very possibility of language as a bearer of truth."
