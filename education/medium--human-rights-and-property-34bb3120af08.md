# Human Rights and Property — core principles

- **Property is the foundational right.** The most fundamental human right is the right to hold property; freedom requires secure control of the integrity of those rights.
- **Bitcoin’s purpose is freedom through property.** The blockchain is about securing ownership, not about shielding crime.
- **Privacy protects ordinary life.** The lack of privacy is not about protecting criminal acts; it is about empowering people to live the life they want to live.
- **Ownership, occupation and use can be separated.** Living in a house does not mean the public needs to know you own it. Tokenised rights can split those roles.
- **Redeemable contracts tokenise property.** Tradable, secure property rights can be abstracted from the individual and from use, then linked on-chain.
- **Shares link to encrypted Metanet records.** Ownership can be divided into tradable shares that point to redeemable contracts stored encrypted and distributed across the Metanet.
- **Voting and revenue can sit on the same structure.** A homeowner pays market-rate rent; rent is distributed pro-rata to the property’s shares; where the homeowner holds 100% of the shares, the money is pseudonymously returned.
