# Scenario 1: Public Registry of an Asset — core principles

- **Assets register on-chain by publishing ownership.** The simplest on-chain asset arrangement: the owner publishes ownership of an asset (e.g. a home) to the blockchain; nothing else is required at the registration stage.
- **Registration is a building block.** Once registered, the asset may be used in subsequent transactions — such as lending constructions — that reference the registry entry.
- **Registry entries are open-ended.** A bare asset registration has no termination date; it is a permanent record, not a time-bound contract.
- **Contracts are state machines.** Real-world legal arrangements can be formalised as finite state machines implementable on the blockchain, built up from the simplest case (bare registration) towards leases, rolling contracts and conditional contracts.
