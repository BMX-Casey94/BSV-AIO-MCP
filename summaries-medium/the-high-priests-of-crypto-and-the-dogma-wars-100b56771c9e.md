# The High Priests of "crypto" and the dogma wars
**Date:** 2020-02-22 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/the-high-priests-of-crypto-and-the-dogma-wars-100b56771c9e
**Subtitle:** Dogma

## Core thesis
A polemic against the "self-appointed" crypto elite whose demand that he perform cryptographic rituals to prove he is Satoshi is recast as religious dogma: his court actions, not their tests, will demonstrate his "role as the sole creator of bitcoin". BTC is denounced as a Ponzi sustained by a priesthood selling wealth-through-holding and the fantasy of taking down government; real decentralisation is a fixed protocol, not a node count; and bitcoin is eminently confiscatable under existing law.

## Key arguments and claims
- The "crypto elite" are "self-appointed authorities with no real knowledge of blockchain" who "have copied my software and broken many of the features"; one principal Core/Ethereum author "before bitcoin in 2012… was close to being homeless and unemployable" and now "begs for money selling the dogma".
- BTC is "the Ponzi that is BTC and associated systems", falsely selling "the concept of wealth through merely holding coins as a system for takedown government and destroy global banking" — the priesthood hierarchy exists to sustain it.
- Decentralisation redefined: "It's decentralised because the platform doesn't change. If bitcoin is not a fixed protocol, it is not decentralised, and hence it is not bitcoin." A variable supply requires manipulation, and "Once this occurs, it is no longer decentralised."
- Lightning is "designed to lose records"; introducing it yields "a system of control by a few individuals". "Bitcoin Core is a partnership… an association that acts in a cult-like manner."
- Confiscatability: bitcoin "can be confiscated and when transactions are more substantial than a few hundred dollars' worth, is the most straightforward system on this planet to be seized"; "Many freezing orders have already been applied across many bitcoin holdings around the world."
- Bitcoin restores property to the digital realm: "the ability to copy files without consequence can be negated and removed using bitcoin"; copyright's digital-age crisis stems from costless copying; "Bitcoin does not remove the traditional aspects of the law that have built society; it enhances them."
- Signatures presuppose identity: "a digital signature algorithm is not a methodology that allows you to create a signature without first having an identity" — and "hundreds of years of common law… associated with pseudonymity and identity" already govern such questions.
- Epistemology of markets: Wikipedia-style pseudo-knowledge relies on a "wisdom of crowds" that only exists "where something is at risk"; bucket-shop traders watching "little squiggly lines" end up "manipulated by more technically market savvy operators".
- Threat and promise: "We have crossed a tipping point this year… I will destroy the myths and then allow the government to destroy every single system that seeks anonymity using a blockchain."
- Refusal of their evidentiary test: "I will never jump through the hoops that your priests want to prove their dogma."

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
The mode is jeremiad: religious vocabulary (priests, dogma, heretic, mass delusion, "cognitive disjunction") is inverted against the "crypto" faithful. Logically he argues by redefinition — decentralisation becomes protocol fixity — and by appeal to the longue durée of common law over technical ritual. Ad hominem is used deliberately to discredit the priesthood's authority rather than answer its demand for a key-signing demonstration. The body shows transcription artefacts ("reinjury bitcoin reintroduces", "thrown under of us"), suggesting speech-to-text drafting.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- "Sign with the genesis key to prove you're Satoshi": rejected outright — authorship and identity are legal facts to be established in court, and a signature scheme cannot create identity where none is established.
- "Many nodes equals decentralisation": denied — fixity of protocol is the criterion, so BTC with SegWit and Lightning is, by his definition, the centralised system.
- "Bitcoin is unconfiscatable and beyond government": freezing orders already exist worldwide, and seizure above trivial amounts is "the most straightforward" on earth.
- HODL/store-of-value orthodoxy: holding-as-wealth is named the Ponzi mechanism of BTC.
- Lightning as scaling saviour: characterised as a record-destroying control structure for a few operators.
