# Equality — core principles

- **Equality of outcome is an impossible goal.** "The only equality we can achieve is one of equal destitution" — humans differ irreducibly in intelligence, aptitude and proclivity, and in an open market higher earnings reflect services that are desired more.
- **Wage signals are the market's natural equaliser.** High wages signal scarcity and attract entrants to the field, so no intervention is needed — "just a free-market without intervention".
- **Failure must be permitted.** "We need to allow people to fail for this is how they learn and develop" — shielding participants from failure destroys the learning mechanism.
- **Running a full verification node is a cost-bearing role, not a right.** "You do not have a right to run a 'full verification node' as this is not a part of Bitcoin as a system. You have the right to pay the costs of running this system" — and hobby node operation should not be subsidised.
- **Capacity limits imposed for equal participation are the equality fallacy applied to protocol policy.** No one has the right to limit Bitcoin's growth to preserve a romanticised idea of running a node at home; holding back scale so all can participate equally is equality of misery as governance.
- **Bitcoin is capitalism embodied.** "Bitcoin is capitalism. Get used to it." — a competitive system, money designed as global cash rather than a speculative "moon" asset.
