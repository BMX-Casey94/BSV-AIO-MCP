# Nobody Asks Where Your Banknote Has Been — core principles

- **Cash is an institution of bounded bearer finality.** Delivery of a token, for value, to a good-faith taker counts as the creation of a fresh, clean title. The institution manufactures a zone in which strangers settle without producing information about where the money came from, and withdraws that protection where the social cost of ignorance becomes intolerable.
- **Bearer cash is memory of quantity and amnesia of provenance.** A provenance-bearing banknote is not a better banknote; it is not a banknote. Attaching a provenance record to a bearer token makes it information-sensitive by construction — building the crisis into the design.
- **The institution never waives genuineness; it economises only on provenance.** A counterfeit passes nothing, at any amount. Registers individuate, and individuation is what money cannot survive. Money is the asset the law has deliberately made unregisterable.
- **Four finalities must be named separately.** Title finality (clean title against prior claimants), obligation finality (discharge of the debt), operational finality (non-reversal within the system), and institutional finality (whether a court or the state later restores the value). Title finality is categorical wherever the currency rule’s conditions are met; institutional finality is probabilistic.
- **Technical control is not legal title.** Operational irreversibility is neither title finality nor institutional finality. Courts act on the person — repayment orders, constructive trusts — while the ledger entry stands. “Blockchain payments are final” is not a proposition but a question: final in which of the four senses?
- **The provenance inquiry the protocol refuses reappears at the edges.** Exchanges and custodians restore it. Permissionless tokens achieve strong operational finality only. Legislatures have declined to place any electronic instrument at the unbounded bearer end.
- **High value works through notice, regulation and forfeiture — not by destroying title.** An unusual all-cash transfer bears on notice; AML duties bind independently of title; forfeiture reproduces the 1758 good-faith-for-value protection. The bound is a pivot, not a slope.
