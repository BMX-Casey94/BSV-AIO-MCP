# Satoshi and the Sophists — core principles

- **Bitcoin is a transparency engine.** Publicity is the disinfectant: the ledger is meant to stamp out dishonesty and fraud, not to hide it.
- **It was not built as underground drug money.** Centralised e-cash such as e-gold and Liberty Reserve already served that purpose, and did it better than any blockchain can.
- **Any headed system can be targeted.** If an individual or a group runs the thing, there is a head that enforcement can cut off.
- **Visible privacy-coin heads are not immune.** Where the operators can be named, they can be punished until those seeking to breach the law move on.
- **Enforcement works by displacement, not cryptographic impossibility.** Pain, not perfect technical immunity, is what moves crime to different pastures.
