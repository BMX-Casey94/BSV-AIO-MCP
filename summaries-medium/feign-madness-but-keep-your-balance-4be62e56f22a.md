# Feign Madness but Keep Your Balance
**Date:** 2019-06-11 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/feign-madness-but-keep-your-balance-4be62e56f22a

## Core thesis
An excerpt framed on stratagem 27 of the Thirty-Six Stratagems (假痴不癲, "feign madness but keep your balance"). Craig claims his erratic public Twitter persona was deliberate misdirection: while opponents watched the performance, he was quietly filing just under 800 patent applications, building a 1,280-white-paper pipeline, pursuing two PhDs, and reaching a sustained ~1,000 transactions per second. The body continues on craigwright.net.

## Key arguments and claims
- The stratagem: "Hide behind the mask of a fool, a drunk, or a madman" so the opponent underestimates you, grows overconfident, drops his guard — "Then you may attack."
- He says he "posted the strategy publicly in 2017" and let people treat "the Twitter persona" as "a reality they should take note of" — the persona was the mask.
- Behind the mask: "we have filed just under 800 patent applications. There are at least 1280 white papers in the pipeline that will lead to patents" — an industrial-scale IP land-grab.
- He is simultaneously "doing" two PhDs: law at the University of Leicester and applied mathematics at CNAM, both "going well".
- Technical claim: "the team has managed to obtain a sustained transaction rate of around 1,000 transactions per second", and "By the end of the year, we will be able to handle a transaction volume the level of Visa."

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Self-mythologising strategy narrative: a classical Chinese military stratagem is applied to his own public relations, converting criticism of his behaviour into proof the deception worked. He then substantiates the "hidden industry" claim with quantitative markers — patent counts, white-paper pipeline, transactions per second — as evidence of what the mask concealed.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Against the open-source, anti-patent ethos of mainstream crypto: he treats an aggressive patent portfolio as an offensive weapon, not a community taboo.
- Against "on-chain can't scale" orthodoxy (post-blocksize-war BTC consensus): he claims 1,000 tx/s sustained and Visa-scale volume imminent on his chain.
- Against proof-of-social-media reputation culture: Twitter perception is a battlefield to be gamed, not a genuine record of intent.
