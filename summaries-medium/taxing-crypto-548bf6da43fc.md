# Taxing Crypto
**Date:** 2019-06-17 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/taxing-crypto-548bf6da43fc
**Subtitle:** Like it or not, Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies do not stop you from paying tax. They are not designed for such a purpose…

## Core thesis
A short excerpt arguing that Bitcoin neither exempts anyone from tax nor was designed to — and that it can instead facilitate fair taxation: a value-added tax can be integrated directly into Bitcoin using script, collected instantly at the point of sale, and made robust even against customer reversals. The body continues on craigwright.net.

## Key arguments and claims
- "Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies do not stop you from paying tax. They are not designed for such a purpose" — tax evasion is outside Bitcoin's design intent.
- "Rather, they can facilitate fair taxation" — compliance is reframed as a protocol feature, not an external imposition.
- "It is possible to integrate a value-added tax directly into Bitcoin using script", which "would allow an organisation to pay its VAT instantly on the sale of goods or services" — real-time remittance at transaction time.
- The design handles edge cases: "even a reversal from the customer would allow instant and automated taxes to still apply without fraud" — refunds/chargebacks do not break the tax logic.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Technical-legal synthesis: he takes a regulatory obligation (VAT) and shows it can be enforced at the script level, turning Bitcoin from a perceived tax-evasion vehicle into tax infrastructure. The argument proceeds by mechanism — what script can express — rather than by policy assertion alone.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Against crypto-anarchist "Bitcoin starves the state" orthodoxy: taxation is not only compatible with Bitcoin but automatable on-chain.
- Against the era's "crypto = offshore tax haven" marketing: he positions Bitcoin as a tool for fair, instant, fraud-resistant tax collection.
- Implicitly against "Bitcoin script is too limited for real applications": VAT logic with reversal handling is proposed as a script-level construct.
