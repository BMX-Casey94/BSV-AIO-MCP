# Micropayments, Immutable Data, and Near-Zero Transaction Costs — core principles

- **Negligible friction ignites new markets.** Micropayments of a hundredth of a cent per provable data exchange, each marked with an immutable evidence trail, drag hidden value into the light and dissolve the Coasean rationale for firms, hierarchies and intermediaries.
- **What is priced is certainty, not bits.** Digital reproduction has near-zero marginal cost; the service being paid for is validation, immutable proof and the guarantee of provenance — the price of verifiable existence.
- **Immutable records kill opportunism.** Cryptographically notarised trails make deception skyrocket in cost; signalling becomes a trivial transaction, opening markets that information asymmetry had previously destroyed.
- **The transaction itself becomes self-enforcing.** Fraud leaves a permanent scar visible to all participants; disputes are pre-empted rather than litigated. Each microtransaction is both performance indicator and payment, annihilating most monitoring costs.
- **Creditworthiness becomes a dynamic reflection of immutable actions.** History is an unbroken series of observable events; Bayesian updating sharpens, and expectation moves from uncertainty toward near-certainty.
- **Individuals become sovereign owners of their digital shadow.** IoT telemetry and user-generated micro-content, once discarded as exhaust, can be monetised directly — users are no longer the product but the traders of their own footprints.
- **When trust is cheap and proof is automatic, every transaction can stand naked under verifiability.** The ledger is the silent judge; the creator has a moral right to price their creation, fracturing rent-seeking giants built on hoarded user data.
