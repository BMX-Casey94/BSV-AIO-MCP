# The Imperative of Scalable Blockchain for Global Commerce — core principles

- **Global commerce needs millions of transactions per second.** A commerce-grade ledger must handle transfers between countries, within countries, between companies and between individuals across 195 markets, each with its own regulations, currencies and systems.
- **The base layer itself must carry world trade.** Sidechains, sharding and cross-chain communication cannot deliver the throughput genuine global commerce demands; scale is not an option layered on later — it is the design requirement.
- **The benchmark is existing international finance.** Stock markets and banks already execute millions of real-time transactions daily; a blockchain that cannot match reliability, scalability and instant execution remains a toy for speculators rather than a tool for real business.
- **One overarching protocol, no intermediary delay.** Instantaneous cross-border trade under a single protocol removes the hours of waiting while a bank approves a transfer. Multi-chain interoperability is a substitute for a ledger that can process all nations, industries and markets itself.
- **Slow speeds, high fees and inefficient consensus are economic decay.** Nations that refuse to adopt a ground-up scalable architecture risk isolation analogous to protectionist autarky; the world will not wait.
- **Teranode-class unbounded scaling is the named engineering path.** Massive investment in infrastructure plus cooperation between businesses, governments and institutions is required — a philosophical as well as a technical choice that transcends national boundaries.
