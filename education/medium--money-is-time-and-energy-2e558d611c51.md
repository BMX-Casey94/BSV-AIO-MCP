# Money Is Time and Energy — core principles

- **Money maps obligations through time.** It is not wealth. Printing or redistributing money cannot create wealth; value is set by the ratio of freely circulating money to available goods and services.
- **A common unit collapses combinatorial barter.** Two merchants each holding three items already face nine direct exchanges; a ledger unit makes multi-party trade workable. Money has formed as a universal societal contract in nearly all human societies.
- **Free money, not total stock, sets the exchange rate.** Money held by people unwilling to move it is effectively unavailable. Inflation follows when money growth outpaces goods growth.
- **Growth can absorb issuance.** If goods grow in step with the money supply, the inflation rate can be zero. The danger is more money chasing fewer goods.
- **Gold’s virtue was discipline, not intrinsic value.** Gold is a marker for other goods. Its value as money lay in the limit it imposed on expansion — a limit undermined by unpredictable new discoveries. Gold was never a cash system for small daily payments.
- **Bitcoin is electronic cash, not a bank.** It does not replace banks. Banks do not replace cash, and Bitcoin does not replace banks. Debt creation belongs to fractional-reserve banking, not to the cash token.
