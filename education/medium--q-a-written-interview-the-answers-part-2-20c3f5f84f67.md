# Q&A/Written Interview — The answers — Part 2 — core principles

- **Bitcoin's identity is technical, not market-assigned.** "Bitcoin is a chain of digital signatures based on ECDSA"; altering block time, reward structure or adding RBF makes a chain no longer Bitcoin. A ticker is merely an exchange mechanism — protocol fidelity, not market capitalisation, defines the system.
- **The protocol was complete in version 0.1.** "Bitcoin was complete in version 0.1" — freeze the base and build on top; "There are no layers outside or off-chain", and full script capability exceeds what generalised world-computer chains were conceived for.
- **Stability has a 500-year precedent.** Pacioli's double-entry bookkeeping has served business essentially unchanged since the 1470s; a money protocol deserves the same conservatism — businesses need assurance the protocol will not change mid-project (multi-year builds such as the SBI threshold-wallet partnership).
- **Proof-of-work is voting with risk, not balances.** "The market is PoW. It is vote with 'skin in the game'… Not one dollar in the bank (PoS), but one dollar risked. Compete or wither."
- **Cash utility is the killer app; speculation adds nothing.** HODL-as-end is "a zero sum game adding no value but allowing open gambling"; the adoption order is cash first, then on-chain contracts, financial instruments and GST/VAT automation.
- **Patents are property rights over implementations.** A patent is "a right to exploit the property you have created"; it requires an implementation, not mere ideas; already-public matter is unpatentable; and applications need real names — "there is no provision for pseudonyms. So, no Satoshi Nakamoto."
- **Worked savings arithmetic shows inflationary banking is not saving.** A £100,000 deposit at 1% with a 30% marginal tax rate yields £700 net; after inflation the real value is £98,260.98 — "You are not saving by placing money into a bank. You just have the convenience." A deflationary-money bank would pay savers under term contracts, "how banking used to work".
- **Permissioned chains are a category error.** "The same as a private permissioned Internet. A joke." — "There is no blockchain, there is Bitcoin."
