# Bitcoin is a commodity — core principles

- **Money has exactly two forms: commodity money and state-decreed fiat.** Bitcoin is not and never will be legal tender, so it must stand as commodity money or fail as "a pure Ponzi" — there is no third category.
- **Bitcoin's commodity is transaction ledger space and computation.** Its value comes from businesses wanting to save data and contracts on an immutable ledger in a manner covered by document-retention law (e.g. US SOX provisions) — use, not exchange speculation, grounds the value.
- **Paid-for data storage is not spam.** "There is no spam on the blockchain that is paid for" — the ledger is a commodity to be bought and used.
- **The scaling economics (2018 Teranode pledge).** Scale Bitcoin to over 4 million transactions per second by 2021 at an average of $0.0025 per transaction, yielding miners over $10,000 per block in fees — fee volume replaces subsidy through use.
- **The legal meaning of money does not require legal tender.** *Travelex Limited v Commissioner of Taxation* [2008] FCA 1961 (at [25]): money is "any generally accepted medium of exchange"; "It is common consent and conduct that gives a thing the character of money" (*Miller v Race* (1758); *Moss v Hancock* [1899]) — reasoning that survived appeal to the Full Federal Court and High Court.
- **Money is abstract, not tangible.** Electronically transferred bank balances are plainly money, so intangibility is no bar to Bitcoin being money; negotiability — transfer of ownership by delivery — is the key feature, and Bitcoin possesses it.
- **US authority recognises Bitcoin as money.** *SEC v Shavers* (E.D. Tex, №4:13-CV-416): Bitcoin "is a currency or form of money" for the investment-contract limb of *Howey*, since it buys goods and services and exchanges for conventional currencies.
- **Bitcoin is intangible personal property.** Per Dr Rhys Bollen, it "involves the circulation of valuable rights but not rights to cash as such… transferred irrevocably and immediately (albeit with delayed confirmation) by electronic order".
