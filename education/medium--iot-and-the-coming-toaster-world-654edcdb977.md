# IoT and the coming Toaster-world — core principles

- **Technology commodification drives infrastructure into the cloud.** Users want toaster-like simplicity and reliability, not control — "we Geeks are a minority and we are losing the war… technology is becoming commodity based."
- **Bitcoin's infrastructure follows the same trajectory.** "The commodification of technology is what will drive the move to the cloud. It is what makes the entire structure of how some see Bitcoin, as a mesh of home nodes incredibly flawed" — the endpoint is "a world, not of hobby Bitcoin nodes, but of commercial enterprise".
- **Bandwidth trends kill local hardware.** With 5G and eventually 10–100Gb/s wireless as a utility, "remote desktops will work faster from server farms"; "When all is available, backed up and secured, who will want or care for a USB device?"
- **Decentralisation is functional, not topological.** What matters is "the ability to have a sound, stable money. A money that is scarce and capped and a money that allows direct peer exchange" — plus a simple immutable ledger letting remote parties trade safely. Node-count topology is not the property users need.
- **Bitcoin is a commercial system by design.** "Bitcoin is designed to be a commercial system, one that is designed to deliver cash to the globe" — being seen as a geek-run system is an adoption problem, because it is money, P2P global cash.
- **Interfaces must reach appliance grade.** "We want an Internet of Toaster like simplicity and reliability… we need to improve the interfaces that people use with Bitcoin" — early steps being consumer wallets such as MoneyButton and HandCash.
- **The ledger becomes a general data layer.** "In years to come, the Bitcoin ledger itself will be a means to interact with and store data" — the chain as data infrastructure, not merely payment rail.
