# The immovable — core principles

- **Bitcoin is a stable protocol.** If you change the protocol, you change it from being Bitcoin.
- **Bitcoin is not anti-bank, anti-government or anti-central-bank.** It cannot be made to be any of those things.
- **The design is an immutable AML trail.** The ledger is meant to give enough privacy that honest use is protected, while making anonymity structurally impossible.
- **A single honest ledger binds governments too.** Unlike SWIFT, which is insecure and simple to bypass, Bitcoin is a record even governments cannot rewrite.
- **Criminal systems are always centralised somewhere.** Liberty Reserve had tens of thousands of fiat on- and off-ramps and still fell; exchanges and their assets remain easy to take down and confiscate.
- **On-chain evidence cannot be deleted.** Wash trading and bucket-shop fraud leave a permanent record that can be taken to court.
- **Scale is the commercial test.** A Bitcoin that can take the world’s commerce — and every other chain — without missing a heartbeat is the system the protocol was built to become.
- **The 2008 bailouts are not the origin.** The design was already in motion in 2007 and basically complete in 2008; it is not a protest coin against Lehman or the subsequent rescue.
