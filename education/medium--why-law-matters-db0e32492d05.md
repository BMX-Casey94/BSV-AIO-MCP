# Why Law Matters — core principles

- **Collectivist “decentralisation” is not a distributed system.** Equating a crowd of home replicas with a distributed protocol is a false equivocation; it relies on people ignoring the tragedy of the commons.
- **Bitcoin does not bring democracy to money.** One-user-one-vote and “everyone can mine equally” are not the design; an equal-access, ASIC-free mining scheme would be a radically different system.
- **Law is the enforcement layer.** Legal process is slow, but it is effective; Bitcoin does not sit beyond courts.
- **Scale the original design.** Build and scale in the way the protocol was originally envisioned — not by inventing a new governance story to replace it.
- **Impersonal validation beats reputation.** Patent examination and blind peer review judge the work, not the person; ad hominem does not settle a technical claim.
