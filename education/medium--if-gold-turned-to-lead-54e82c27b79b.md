# If Gold Turned to Lead — core principles

- **Bitcoin is an immutable evidence trail.** Its novelty is tracing — a key aspect of money that earlier systems ignored. It is not about taking down governments.
- **Private, not anonymous.** The system records everything and can be tracked and traced forever, remaining private but not anonymous.
- **A key does not demonstrate ownership.** Possession of a private key is not title. Courts can order seizure; “keep your keys so no one can control your bitcoin” is not how property works.
- **Stolen value can be made useless.** The design idea is a remote kill switch: stolen gold that turns to lead until returned — crime should not pay.
- **Freezing on a valid judicial order remains in Bitcoin.** The August 2010 alert system allowed nodes to freeze assets globally on receipt of a validly issued judicial order.
- **Distribution is robustness, not anarchy.** Nodes are not there to deliver decentralisation. The system is distributed so that when a company fails, Bitcoin does not. Mining professionalises into data-centre companies under law.
- **There was never a goal of money outside government.** The anarchist catchcry is inimical to the purpose of honest money. There is no way to create a system like Bitcoin that does not eventually end constrained by law.
- **Criminal proceeds leave a court-admissible record.** Any criminal who has received bitcoin has left a record that is admissible in court; freezing orders and proceeds-of-crime cases will link bitcoin and tokenised assets to crime.
