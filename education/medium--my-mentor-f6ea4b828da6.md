# My Mentor — core principles

- **Code is not law and never will be.** Treating a key signature as self-sufficient evidence, and seeking to remove courts, judges and law, is the path Bitcoin was built to leave behind.
- **Two-of-three multi-signature escrow is native Bitcoin.** It was in the original version; escrow was never about removing courts and jurisdiction.
- **Encode courts into the contract.** Parties can automate the selection of jurisdictional functions — courts, arbitrators and regulators — as keys inside the script rather than abolishing them.
- **Compile contract rules into key selection.** Below a value threshold the local court and magistrate key is used; above it, a District Court key as the rules require — selection automated by contract value.
- **Oracles plus court certificates automate procedure.** Contracts can apply administrative rules of civil procedure or securities-offering rules in a given jurisdiction by accepting certified court or oracle outputs.
- **Merklised alternative paths route issues to the right forum.** Consumer-law questions can go to a tribunal key and equity issues to a court-of-equity key, all within a simplified transaction.
- **Do not remove lawyers; automate some of their functions.** Smart contracts should incorporate regulators, arbitrators and courts, not route around them.
