# Patent wars… — core principles

- **SPV is defined in section 8 of the white paper.** Users keep block headers of the longest proof-of-work chain and verify the Merkle branch — a Bayesian query system, not a broken shortcut.
- **Sidechains save nothing unless they destroy records.** A million transactions on a sidechain create as much data as a million on a single chain; the only saving is periodic deletion of history.
- **Accounts without a history are a laundering feature.** A parallel chain that may be deleted periodically opens the ability to engage in illicit activity; that is the differentiator, not scale.
- **Lawful commerce needs retained records.** Share transfers, tokenised securities and tax receipts must be maintained, so they cannot run on deletable sidechains.
- **Bitcoin is an honest-marketplace machine.** Built after audit-firm work, it reduces corporate fraud, tax fraud and general account fraud by making the record immutable.
- **Scale on the base layer, in terabyte blocks.** A micro-services node (iDaemon / Teranode) is designed to take 10-minute blocks into terabytes and eventually petabytes, putting all commerce on one immutable system.
- **Protocol tinkering delays a scaled node.** A protocol designed to be fixed and set in stone, once repeatedly modified, puts a working large-block node years behind.
- **Amount-concealment fails commercial AML rules.** Cryptography that hides ledger amounts would not meet MLD5-style updates; strict-liability possession offences destroy the usefulness of anonymous digital cash.
- **Bitcoin is pseudo-anonymous, not anonymous.** That distinction is why activity on a visible ledger can be investigated; it is a design property, not a defect.
