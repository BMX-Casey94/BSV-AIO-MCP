# Scenario 5: Contract Conditionality — core principles

- **Contracts can embed conditions precedent on-chain.** A construction contract with a pool of builders specifies a number of conditions that gate progression; each condition becomes a guarded state transition.
- **Counterparties can be collective.** The contracting party can be a pool — multiple builders — rather than a single entity.
- **Third-party attestation is a first-class transition.** Independent sign-off, such as planning-authority approval of the plans, gates the contract's advance; real-world institutions participate in the state machine as attesters (the oracle pattern).
- **On-chain contracts complement the legal system.** The model incorporates existing authorities into the workflow rather than displacing them — contracts on Bitcoin work with legal institutions, not around them.
