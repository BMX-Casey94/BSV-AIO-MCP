# Accountability Follows Control: English Private Law and the Governance of Bitcoin — core principles

- **Responsibility follows control as a matter of doctrine, not policy.** Where governance over Bitcoin is exercised in fact — collectively, informally, without incorporation — yet produces binding outcomes relied upon by others, English private law already classifies that governance as accountable; the "accountability gap" is not a gap in the law but a gap in admission.
- **Control is proved factually, not rhetorically.** Evidential indicators: gatekeeping (deciding what is accepted, rejected, implemented, enforced), agenda-setting (defining which questions may be considered at all), veto power, and persistence plus coordination over time. Influence persuades; authority decides.
- **Partnership doctrine classifies informal collective governance.** Under the Partnership Act 1890 classification turns on conduct, not paperwork or subjective intention; only those exercising legally material control are partners — mere contributors, users and peripheral participants are not.
- **"Business in common" and "view to profit" are read realistically.** Continuous, coordinated governance producing authoritative outcomes satisfies business in common; profit is read broadly to include strategic positioning, preferential access to information or opportunities, and reputational capital translatable into economic opportunity.
- **Mutual agency follows automatically from partnership.** Every partner is an agent of the firm, so rule-setting and enforcement decisions are acts of the firm itself; authority may be actual (inferred from repeated practice and recognised roles) or ostensible (holding out plus reasonable reliance).
- **Joint control requires no hierarchy.** English law has never required a single controlling mind; veto power and mutual constraint demonstrate shared authority, and joint control attracts joint responsibility.
- **Fiduciary duty is positional, not moral.** Discretionary power over others' interests, power asymmetry and reliance trigger equity's strictness irrespective of honesty or good faith; fiduciary doctrine reaches where negligence cannot, because negligence is constrained by proximity and pure-economic-loss limits.
- **Remedies are orthodox and operational.** Constructive trust, tracing, account of profits (gain-based, not loss-based), equitable compensation, and interim relief restraining contested decisions or dissipation pending trial; proprietary remedies confer priority over unsecured creditors and can survive insolvency.
- **The sequence is fixed.** Control triggers classification; classification gives rise to duty; duty attracts remedy — labels do not decide liability, structure does not erase duty, and voluntary participation is not a waiver of accountability. English private law has never permitted authority to exist in a responsibility vacuum.
