# Where Your Treasure Is — core principles

- **Money is judged by what it builds.** Stewardship is risk, labour and creation — the multiplication of talents. Passive speculation is the burial of talent: it produces no good, hires no labourer, funds no poor, and creates no community.
- **Wealth must move.** Capital must be applied to justice, inclusion and creation. To hold, in the true sense, is to steward; to hoard is to rot. Digital assets, if bought, should fund innovation, not speculation.
- **Engineered scarcity is not virtue.** Virtue does not lie in a fixed cap; it lies in righteous distribution, abundant giving and generous multiplication. “Buy early, persuade others to join late, and exit when the price allows me to abandon work” is pyramid logic, not trade.
- **Extraction without contribution is theft.** Creation is divine; wealth gained hastily dwindles. The system’s worth is circulation that funds work, not a theology of windfall without labour.
- **Electronic cash is for use, not accumulation.** The redemptive model is shared enterprise that liberates, not bigger wallets and more keys in total inertia.
