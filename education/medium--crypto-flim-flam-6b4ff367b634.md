# Crypto flim-flam — core principles

- **Decentralisation means the decentralisation of power.** A token and an exchange run by named controllers are not decentralised, whatever the marketing says.
- **There is no group-owned decentralised exchange.** Every ICO-style venue is controlled by individuals and remains subject to law — including US law on a .com domain.
- **AML covers the mythical DEX too.** Exchanges that cannot report historical suspicious activity will not be able to trade lawfully; operating without a complete AML/KYC programme is criminal.
- **Value is what is offered to society.** Markets come through use and final consumption, not through the ticker on a bucket-shop exchange.
- **Exchange price is not the market.** Bitcoin does not have value because of those venues; they are Main Street, not the measure of the protocol.
- **“Democratisation of finance” is an old securities pitch.** Promising households riches without the research institutions do is the vocabulary of bucket shops, not a new monetary design.
- **One protocol survives by use.** After the crash of pumped tokens, the ledger that is actually used — not the one that is merely traded — is the one that remains.
