# Tax and Bitcoin — Investment in Bitcoin — core principles

- **Investment in Bitcoin is taxed like any other convertible emolument.** What can reasonably be regarded as money is taxed under the same provisions as any other investment; no new law is required.
- **Mining is a competitive business.** It only makes sense when done efficiently and for a profit; mining contracts are used in securing the network.
- **Profit-seeking mining expenses are deductible.** Where the taxpayer has the objective of deriving income in the future, expenditure in gaining or producing assessable income is allowable — the same rule as for ordinary business.
- **Trading bitcoin is analogous to foreign-exchange trading.** It should be taxed under the same forex provisions; dealers hold instruments as trading stock and realisation gains are ordinary income.
- **Disposal can fall within the traditional-securities regime.** Gains on redemption or disposal of instruments treated as traditional securities are already assessable under existing statute.
- **Nothing about Bitcoin resists existing categories.** Mining, trading and disposal each already have a taxing regime with appellate authority.
