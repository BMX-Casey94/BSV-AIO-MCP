# Dynamic disequilibria and the creation of criminal opportunity — core principles

- **Crime and security co-evolve as coupled markets.** "The security industry and criminal activity feeds upon itself. Crime and entrepreneurship each create opportunities for the other" — every security innovation spawns new criminal innovation, and new criminal groups open markets for entrepreneurial security firms.
- **Market discipline applies to criminal innovation.** Where crime is insufficiently profitable for its risk, innovation is driven down and incumbent criminal groups "entrench and act to protect their existing market share" — raising risk and cutting profitability suppresses new forms of crime.
- **Disequilibria breed opportunity; stagnation is symmetric.** Concurrent disequilibria from innovation inevitably create openings in legitimate and black markets alike; "In long term states of relatively low disequilibria, all societies stagnate. This is equally true of crime."
- **Perfection is not an available engineering target.** "No system is ever perfect. No system can be made to be perfect. What we need to do is no more than to ensure that a system is not economically viable to be exploited."
- **0-conf needs risk management, not a protocol fix.** "We do not need to find a 'Fix' for 0-conf, it is already secure enough. We only need to ensure that we understand the risk we face."
- **A commercial money system manages risk rather than redesigning for it.** "Bitcoin is a commercial system. It is money" — risk is best mitigated "not by altering the system, but in the creation of the best responses to risk. Some risk will be mitigated, some shall be accepted. This is the nature of risk."
