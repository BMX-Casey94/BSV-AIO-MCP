# The Quiet Violence of Sunday — core principles

- **A node is an economic actor who creates blocks.** In the original design a node competes in the business of producing blocks — produce, or fall behind. A spectator archive offers participation without work and control without consequence.
- **Observation is not contribution; validation is not governance.** Running software and consuming blocks is not authority. Only actors who bear cost and risk enforce rules through economic action.
- **Fixing the rules dissolves the tinker's office.** The moment the protocol is set in stone, the authority of those who revise it evaporates. Stability is the death of a priesthood that must canonise instability to remain indispensable.
- **Digital cash's purpose is micropayments, rapid settlement, lawful privacy and traceability.** Real privacy is traceable. Anonymous systems are not private; they are deceptive. Anonymity without responsibility was never the design.
- **Miners hold property rights grounded in reliance.** The ledger is common-law property. The public representation that the protocol is set in stone gives rise to promissory estoppel — promise, reliance, detriment — once capital is invested on that assurance.
- **Semantic collapse is how capture hides.** "Layer 2" for networks that never reconnect, "scaling" as a posture of restraint, "digital gold" as a costume for the absence of function — euphemisms adopted after the failure to function as cash.
- **A system that does always outlasts a system that merely declares.** Slogans and Sunday doctrine are not engineering proofs; reality is the final arbiter.
