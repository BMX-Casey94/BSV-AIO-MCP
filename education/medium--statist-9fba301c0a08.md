# Statist — core principles

- **Bitcoin is an immutable evidence system.** It "provides the rule of law universally to all. It applies to people, corporations, and the state" — evidentiary infrastructure for honest government, not a tool to abolish it.
- **A framework alone is insufficient.** Evidence on-chain does not replace vigilance: "all people need to be vigilant."
- **Capitalism requires law and a limited state.** The core function of that state is protecting private property and peace. Liberalism is not anarchism, "nor has it anything whatsoever to do with anarchism" (Mises).
- **Crime is not contractual.** Competing private courts let the accused shop for jurisdiction, so "the rich and the powerful exceed the right of the victim for redress" — an encoded axiom of "Might is right."
- **Codification and adjudication are government.** If all members of a society subscribe to different and possibly conflicting courts and laws, "then there is no law." The anarchist who calls for private courts has reinvented the state while denying it.
- **Monopoly is managed, not abolished.** It "exists in every system, it is not something to be expunged completely, but something to be managed" for optimal efficiency.
- **Corruption is stopped by participation, not abolition.** Government is like a house: left unmaintained it rots. "It is not the abolition of the state that will set us free, it is the involvement of people in the state."
