# Bitcoin After the Casino — core principles

- **Bitcoin is an economic machine for creating, transferring and verifying value, state, authority and evidence between parties.** Its deeper achievement is digital scarcity at the level of individual transactions — unique spendable states that move rather than multiply. Profit comes from production and exchange, not from resale to a later buyer.
- **The protocol is a commitment, not a living document.** Businesses do not invest for thirty or fifty years on infrastructure whose rules a committee can rewrite. Restoration of the unrestricted original design ends the programme of protocol alteration; it does not begin another one.
- **Protocol is not implementation.** Node software may improve indefinitely without changing Bitcoin. A node that changes the rules has ceased to be an honest node. Fixedness moves innovation to implementations, applications and overlays — as a stable grammar enables poetry, contracts and science.
- **The white paper title is a specification.** Peer-to-peer means parties transact directly, not everyone keeping identical copies. Electronic cash means transferable value with cash-like immediacy and evidential character.
- **UTXOs are discrete, single-use state objects.** Spending consumes prior outputs and creates successors, so recipients hold evidence — transaction, chain of title, signatures, inclusion proof — rather than trusting a platform's database entry. That shift from platform permission to possessed evidence is the beginning of digital property.
- **SPV is the overlooked half of the design.** Miners perform industrial ordering while users verify only what concerns them. Hierarchical Merkle commitments keep proof growth logarithmic; proofs travel with the payment. Everyone-stores-everything suppresses scale, raises costs and pushes ordinary users into custodial services.
- **Transactional scarcity is not merely twenty-one million coins.** Tickets, licences, credentials, invoices, warranties, contractual rights and financial instruments transfer without leaving a valid copy behind. Ownership moves rather than multiplies.
- **The sustainable mining model is high volume multiplied by low fees.** The subsidy was never a permanent business model. Restricting block space prices out ordinary payments, manufactures rent for intermediaries, and forces reliance on speculation.
- **Success is measured in transactions, not market capitalisation.** Reconciliation collapses as payment, performance, title and evidence are linked; fraction-of-a-cent transactions open markets below today's minimum economic size. Privacy with accountability; anonymity attempts to remove identity and responsibility. Bitcoin should become boring.
