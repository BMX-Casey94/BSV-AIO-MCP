# Resilience, Redundancy, and Real-World Data Integrity — core principles

- **Immutability guarantees permanence, not truth.** A blockchain is a scribe, not a lie detector. If sensors lie, the ledger immortalises the lie with cryptographic certainty. Garbage in, garbage immutably logged.
- **Truth must be engineered before recording.** Triple-redundant sensing — one sensor is a gamble, two a quarrel, three a quorum — plus reconciliation that discards the outlier, so only a verified reading is signed and broadcast.
- **Decide offline; broadcast when a path exists.** Minimalist devices pre-sign transactions, sleep, wake on solar or timed cycles, and emit via mule, radio or satellite. Deciding is decoupled from broadcasting.
- **Scheduled micropayments bind intention to outcome.** A transaction is a container of command: release a volume at a sat-per-litre rate from pre-approved credit; stop if a voltage threshold fails. Templates are flexible contracts signed in advance.
- **Stablecoins are denominated utility overlays.** They settle on a chain capable of millions of micropayments per second; the farmer need never know block size or hash rate.
- **The loop closes on-chain.** Post-action deltas are measured and the next round adjusts — recursive refinement, not AI. Every drop is accounted for immutably and queryable by block height.
- **Plain pre-signed Script beats theatrical contracts.** Verification precedes recording; low-power minimalism is discipline. A ledger cannot lie, but it can be told untruths — so the honest chain is owed honest inputs.
