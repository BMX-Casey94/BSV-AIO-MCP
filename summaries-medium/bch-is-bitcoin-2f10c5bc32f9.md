# BCH is Bitcoin.
**Date:** 2018-09-02 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/bch-is-bitcoin-2f10c5bc32f9

## Core thesis
The entire body of this post is a single declarative sentence — "BCH is Bitcoin." — published with no supporting argument. Its content is the assertion itself: the Bitcoin Cash chain, not BTC, is the legitimate continuation of the system Satoshi described. Read alongside the same day's companion posts, the claim rests on the cash-not-gold definition of Bitcoin and the original-protocol fidelity argument developed elsewhere in the batch.

## Key arguments and claims
- The post makes one claim and offers zero reasoning: the totality of the text is the title sentence restated as the body. Any argument for it must be inferred from context and from Craig's surrounding essays.
- Implicit claim (from context): legitimacy of the name "Bitcoin" attaches to the chain that preserves the original design — peer-to-peer electronic cash with the v0.1.0 protocol locked — which he argues BTC abandoned (see "Banking on Bitcoin", same day: "Bitcoin core (BTC) should not even be called bitcoin anymore. It is not cash.").
- Implicit claim (from context): BCH's restoration of original opcodes and on-chain scaling makes it the heir to the whitepaper system — the position argued in "Limited change to bring stability" ("Bitcoin cash is bitcoin and we're going to work to return it to the version 0.1.0 implementation and lock that protocol").
- Timing is the message: posted 2 September 2018, ten weeks before the BCH/BSV split of 15 November 2018, when Craig's camp (nChain/CoinGeek) was still fighting for the BCH ticker and brand rather than a separate BSV identity.

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
There is no reasoning in the text itself; the post is a pure performative assertion — a branding act rather than an essay. In Craig's wider method this functions as the conclusion stated without its premises, with the premises supplied by the surrounding corpus (original-protocol fidelity, cash-as-defining-purpose, stability-through-immutability). As rhetoric it is a loyalty test and search-engine claim-stake on the word "Bitcoin".

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Frontally contradicts the BTC-mainstream consensus that the BTC chain is Bitcoin — the ticker, brand and network-effect inheritance are asserted for BCH instead.
- Implicitly denies that market price, hash-majority or exchange tickers confer legitimacy; legitimacy is defined by protocol fidelity to the original design (the argument made explicit in his other 2018 posts).
- Within months this same absolutist naming logic was turned against BCH itself: after the November 2018 split his camp declared BSV the true bearer ("Bitcoin is cash and it does not change (BSV)" — "The crypto-ring of Gyges", same day), showing the claim was factional rather than chain-specific.
