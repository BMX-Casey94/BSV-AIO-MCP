# Decentralised planning — core principles

- **No planner can predict society.** No person, department or body can foresee the course society will take; a market outcome is the view of society, and a few public servants are not society.
- **Central planning is a recurring failed experiment.** It returns because those in government have strong incentives to keep government, and its failures, in place.
- **Bitcoin removes power from money.** It is not about miners, developers or exchanges; the underlying basis of money should not be about control.
- **A stable monetary base is like gold.** It is a base for measuring and transferring value on which other systems can be built.
- **The supply cap is a primary rule.** Altering the 21 million limit is an attempt to change the nature of Bitcoin and capture power over it.
- **A fixed protocol eliminates fork messiness.** With the protocol set, there is no governance by fork; there is one Bitcoin, and there can only ever be one.
- **Bitcoin is cash and a commodity, not currency.** Currency is something issued and controlled by government; a currency can be built as a tokenised system on top of Bitcoin.
- **Control of the protocol creates legal liability.** Those who can alter the rules have power over the system and are liable for consequences covered by the scope of law.
