# Satoshi’s Vision: The Art of Bitcoin
**Date:** 2019-09-12 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/satoshis-vision-the-art-of-bitcoin-a793a274a64b
**Subtitle:** The book ‘Satoshi’s Vision: The Art of Bitcoin’ is the product of a compilation of a selected number of Craig’s blog posts published on…

## Core thesis
This is a short promotional announcement, not an argumentative essay: it announces the book *Satoshi’s Vision: The Art of Bitcoin* (Howson Books), a compilation of selected posts from Craig's Medium and craigwright.net blogs, lightly re-edited so the collection reads as "one story that is larger than the sum of its parts". The remainder of the body is purchase links and a publisher's disclaimer.

## Key arguments and claims
- The book anthologises "a selected number of Craig's blog posts published on Medium and craigwright.net" into a single volume.
- Editorial method: content is "for the most part taken as it was in the original posts", though "some parts have been reedited and repurposed" to give the reader clarity — the aim being a single narrative larger than its parts.
- The framing itself is the claim: dispersed blog output is repackaged as a curated, canonical statement of "Satoshi's Vision" during his 2019 public campaign to be recognised as Bitcoin's author.
- Publisher's disclaimer: "The opinions expressed by Howson Books, it's editors, or affiliates shall not, in the book or elsewhere, reflect the opinion expressed by Craig Wright"; no guarantee of accuracy or completeness; "Nothing in the book constitutes legal, financial, or other professional advice."
- Honest note: the post contains no substantive argument about Bitcoin — it is a book announcement with links (books2read.com, his Medium profile, craigwright.net).

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Not applicable in the usual sense — this is marketing copy rather than an essay. Its significance is presentational: the 2018–19 blog corpus is repositioned as a coherent book-length canon under the "Satoshi's Vision" banner, with an arm's-length publisher disclaimer that keeps legal distance between the publisher and Craig's claims.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- No direct engagement: the post argues nothing itself. Indirectly, the very title asserts ownership of the "Satoshi's Vision" label at a time when the BTC mainstream denied his authorship — the book is the artefact of that contested claim.

## Notable quotes
- "The content is for the most part taken as it was in the original posts, with the aim of creating, in one book, one story that is larger than the sum of its parts."
- "The opinions expressed by Howson Books, it's editors, or affiliates shall not, in the book or elsewhere, reflect the opinion expressed by Craig Wright."
- "Nothing in the book constitutes legal, financial, or other professional advice."

## Connections
The book anthologises the very Medium/craigwright.net essays this corpus summarises (2018–19 era). Published September 2019, weeks after the adverse sanction findings in the Kleiman litigation and in the middle of his Satoshi-authorship media campaign.
