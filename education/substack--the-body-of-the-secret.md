# The body of the secret — core principles

- **A private key is a physical secret with a location.** Cryptographic proofs do not abolish matter. The instant a key is used it re-enters the physical world as charge, voltage, time, heat and sound, and the physical world leaks.
- **Bitcoin makes the key a bearer instrument of absolute purity.** To lose the key is to lose the value outright, with no registrar to petition. Disintermediation strips the bank's protection along with its permission, raising custody's stakes to the absolute.
- **Practical attack breaks matter, not mathematics.** Timing, differential power analysis, electromagnetic radiation, acoustic leakage, fault injection and cold-boot recovery all leave the cipher intact and extract the key from the device that holds it.
- **Trusted hardware concentrates the physical problem; it does not abolish it.** Tamper-resistant is an honest word; tamper-proof is not. Enclave attestation, chased to its origin, dissolves into the fab, the foundry, the supply chain and the courier.
- **The chain of trust bottoms out in artifacts and hands.** Thompson's regress does not terminate in a proof. The last keeper of every secret is a coercible human body; biometrics cannot be revoked once lifted.
- **Honest security is expensive, accountable and bounded.** Design as though the adversary already holds the device; minimise the secret and its embodied life; split secrets across many bodies so no single chip, disk, person or room is the entire key; name the physical root.
- **A trust surface unnamed is a trust surface unguarded.** Security in the abstract is perfect and worthless. The honest claim is never that a secret cannot be taken, but that taking it has been made expensive.
