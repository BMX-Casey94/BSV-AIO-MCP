# Taxing Crypto — core principles

- **Bitcoin does not exempt anyone from tax.** It was not designed for tax evasion, and using it does not remove tax obligations.
- **Compliance can be built into the protocol layer.** Bitcoin can facilitate fair taxation rather than frustrate it.
- **VAT can be integrated directly into Bitcoin using Script.** Tax logic is expressible as a script-level construct, not only as an external accounting process.
- **Script-based tax enables real-time remittance.** An organisation can pay its VAT instantly on the sale of goods or services — collection at transaction time rather than in arrears.
- **Edge cases are handleable in-script.** Even a reversal from the customer can allow instant and automated taxes to still apply without fraud — refunds and chargebacks do not break the tax logic.
- **Design principle.** Treat regulatory obligations as mechanism-design requirements: what Script can express, the ledger can enforce, turning Bitcoin into tax and audit infrastructure.
