# Sustaining Hash
**Date:** 2018-11-13 | **URL:** https://medium.com/@craig_10243/sustaining-hash-50cad6c16c4b
**Subtitle:** Many out there, such as the developers in ABC want you to believe that a burst of hash matters. It is promoted that ABC have a burst…

## Core thesis
Written two days before the BCH/BSV split, this is Craig's hash-war doctrine: rented "bursts" of hash power — which he claims Bitcoin ABC planned with Bitmain's support — cannot win, because "what matters is sustained hash. Not over a day or even a week, but months." SV's side has more sustained, long-term hash (plus allies), will mine at a loss for the long term, and will 51%-attack and "tear down" any BCH chain that is not the original white paper Bitcoin: "We do not forgive. We do not forget."

## Key arguments and claims
- The target: "developers in ABC want you to believe that a burst of hash matters. It is promoted that ABC have a burst planned with Bitmain's support, that this will make them the longest chain and win." (The essay's image caption mocks "ABC showing us the power of CTOR".)
- "This is NOT how Bitcoin works. What matters is sustained hash. Not over a day or even a week, but months."
- Rented hash is transient: "you take a short-term investment, and spend tens of millions on rented hash. This gives you a burst. Then, as fast as it happened, it is over." Real commitment means purchasing machines; "The only way to keep gains in Bitcoin is the long term."
- "In a hash competition, it matters that you run a marathon, not a sprint."
- SV's position: sustained machines already mining at a level "not profitable to maintain... yet, we will for the long-term gains. We are not Keynesian, we are here for Bitcoin Cash and ONLY BCH." Allies stay because "long term we will win. Not as we will fight a day, but we will not end."
- The attack commitment: "as soon as you stop, we have 51%, and we do not agree with any chain in BCH that is NOT the original white paper's version of Bitcoin."
- War-of-attrition economics: if ABC bursts, SV will "just sit on BTC and sell back for fiat money. Then, as soon as you stop, we take all you tried to hold and tear your dishonest chain down."
- Consequences of losing 51%: "you lose all you have dishonestly taken. Every false block, every stolen transaction and illicit exchange, it all goes to zero" — chain reorganisations will reverse settled payments, since miners' funds take time to settle.
- The demand: be willing to spend money attacking Bitcoin Cash "for years, not months, not days, but YEARS... We will wear you down."

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
Game-theoretic deterrence through a declared war of attrition: he reframes hash competition from a single-shot longest-chain race into a stamina contest where time preference decides — "We are not Keynesian" is his jab at opponents' alleged short-termism. The logic is credible-commitment signalling: publicise sunk costs (unprofitable sustained mining), promise indefinite escalation, and make the opponent's continued defence irrational. Rhetorically it is a threat letter, closing by appropriating Anonymous's slogan ("We do not forgive. We do not forget.") — ironic given the "Corporate Activism" essay posted three days earlier attacking Anonymous.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
- Contradicts the standard Nakamoto-consensus story that the longest/heaviest chain simply *is* legitimate and forks resolve peacefully: Craig asserts a right and duty to attack a majority chain he deems "dishonest" until it conforms to "the original white paper's version of Bitcoin".
- Contradicts "hash rate follows profit": he openly advocates mining at a loss indefinitely for ideological ends, treating hash as a weapon rather than an economic good.
- Contradicts the "one CPU one vote / miners serve the network" ethos: miners here are partisan enforcers who decide which rules may exist — continuous with "Corporate Activism" ("We will use hash, and then we will use law").
- Contradicts the immutability-for-everyone framing: he promises deliberate chain reorganisations to reverse "every false block, every stolen transaction" on the opposing chain.
