# Looking the Other Way — core principles

- **Bitcoin is designed to promote legitimate exchange.** It is easily controlled by law. An immutable ledger that traces money while allowing privacy makes criminal activity more expensive, and as the cost of crime rises, the amount of crime falls.
- **Scale is part of the design.** Not only megabytes of data, but scale and price: throughput and value that recapture the original economic framework of electronic cash and commercial web systems, including micropayments.
- **Bitcoin is not controlled by the users.** No blockchain is. A node is defined in section 5 of the white paper; only point 3 references solving a hash puzzle.
- **An immutable ledger can replace low-level audit labour.** Computer-assisted audit technology on a permanent record is how the system was meant to serve tax authorities and legitimate commerce.
- **User-node narratives do not define the network.** Commercial miners, not a widely distributed set of user machines, are the nodes that matter.
