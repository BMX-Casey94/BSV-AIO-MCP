# The Arithmetic of the Last Fool — core principles

- **Value must be created before it is stored.** A farm produces crops, a bond promises cash flows, a share is a residual claim. A token that does not scale, serve commerce or carry ordinary transactions is storing belief, not value.
- **Buyer-dependence is the defining economic category.** A system becomes Ponzi-like when present holders depend primarily on future buyers rather than present utility, productive yield or transactional necessity.
- **Throughput is the exit as well as the entrance.** At a handful of transactions per second the network is a turnstile. Liquidity is proven when everyone wants out, not in; congestion with a logo is not freedom.
- **Scarcity without use is not money.** Scarcity matters only when joined to utility, beauty, status, necessity, productivity or power. A dead rat in a locked box is scarce.
- **Real economies run on commercial records.** Invoices, shipping manifests, purchase orders, customs records, refunds, audits and receipts are the work. A system that cannot carry them — or labels logistics, EDI, data, micropayments and enterprise records as spam — is a speculative ornament.
- **Store-of-value-first cannot complete.** If the answer to "why hold?" is "because someone else will pay more", the system is buyer-dependent; if "because it enables commerce", it must demonstrate commerce at scale.
- **Cheap high-volume trial is how businesses choose a rail.** A network that treats business transactions as pollution will not become the foundation of business. Respectability, custody products and conferences do not create utility.
