# Drugs, Fraud and Murder — core principles

- **Bitcoin is Main Street cash, not a casino.** It is honest peer-to-peer money operating within law, not a permission-less betting layer or a dark-alley instrument.
- **Pseudonymous by design; privacy excludes anonymity.** Privacy is required for a working legal system; the entire concept of blockchains is a system built on law — contracts need provable consideration and records recoverable across time.
- **Bitcoin is not permission-less.** Nothing you create will be; law remains, and code is just evidence in law.
- **Miners are common carriers until they are not.** Existing Script keeps miners safe because they merely forward valid cash transactions; a purpose-designed opcode created to facilitate unlicensed betting makes miners and pools facilitators, individually responsible for the blocks they accept.
- **Cash is fungible; a betting contract is not.** An on-chain datasig contract used as a bucket shop is not cash; a licensed-gaming PKI version could exist but has no need to live in Bitcoin.
- **The ledger never forgets.** Facilitation leaves a permanent record; statutes of limitation are long, and proceeds remain recoverable.
- **Law reaches the opcode.** Releasing code onto a blockchain does not absolve anyone from complying with pertinent laws — the key word is facilitating.
