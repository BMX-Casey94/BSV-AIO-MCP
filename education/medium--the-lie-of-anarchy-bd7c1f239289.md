# The lie of anarchy — core principles

- **Bitcoin was designed to promote the rule of law, not to abolish the state.** It is "a system where all are equal under the law and sunlight is shone on the corruption that festers in the dark" — built for engagement with sound government, not escape from it.
- **Immutability is a compliance primitive.** SOX §§802 and 1102 (18 U.S.C. 1519; 18 U.S.C. 1512(c)) criminalise destroying evidence relevant to actual or even contemplated federal investigations — "set the basis for why an immutable blockchain is needed".
- **The Enron counterfactual.** "If the accounts of Enron had been implemented on a blockchain-based ledger, there would have been no way for these to be destroyed"; SOX §409 real-time disclosure of legal risks becomes simple with on-chain records at scale.
- **Immutable records solve litigation readiness.** Parties must list not only documents they hold but those "that once existed and have been destroyed" — an incredibly difficult task that an immutable ledger answers by construction.
- **Evidentiary presumption drives design.** "If you cannot prove it using evidence, then there is a presumption that you have not done anything" — Bitcoin lets organisations both meet security needs and prove they have done so.
- **Audit theory: measurement enables management.** The auditor's primary objective is to measure and report on risk; the auditor is "the eyes and ears of management" — an unalterable ledger is the measurement substrate.
- **The CIA triad is preserved in Bitcoin.** Confidentiality, integrity and availability map onto the protocol; compliance regimes (PCI-DSS, Australia's CLERP9 — "as onerous as, if not more onerous than SOX" — and criminal penalties around Tax File Numbers and SSNs) create enterprise demand for provable retention.
- **Automated compliance is the application.** Machine learning over on-chain records — document classification, keyword data-mining of warehouses, database isolation rules, network scanning — turns disclosure into an automated control.
- **Bitcoin's real business purpose.** "It is not anarchist money, it is sound reporting, a system that is resistant to corruption and a means to allow corporations to be more efficient."
