## Problem addressed
Tracing and seizing digital assets on a public chain is described as convoluted; illicit use, illegal content and unwanted personal data can sit in transactions. Nodes and wallets need a way to whitelist or blacklist addresses, UTXOs, transaction identifiers or payload content, and to refuse to sign, mine, validate or serve matching transactions, without distributing huge explicit lists that are slow, large and privacy-leaking.

## Core mechanism
A transmitting party (for example a court or other authority) builds one or more probabilistic filters (Bloom or Cuckoo are described) that encode whitelist or blacklist sets, and sends the filters to a receiving party (user or node). The receiver obtains a transaction and a candidate item — destination address, referenced UTXO, transaction identifier or content such as data after OP_RETURN — and tests membership. Filters have no false negatives: an item is either definitely absent or possibly present. The receiver then decides whether to sign, accept for publication, validate a containing block, or transmit the transaction. Size figures in the specification show large compression versus raw address or content lists. A node may keep the filter in memory for a fast test and a full list or authority query for confirming possible false positives.

## Independent claims (gist — not legal scope)
Claim 1 is a receiving-party method: obtain probabilistic filter(s) encoding whitelist or blacklist sets; obtain a blockchain transaction associated with a candidate item from those sets; and decide whether to process the transaction according to whether the candidate is present in a filter. Claim 13 is a transmitting-party method of generating those filters and sending them to a receiving party. Claims 14 and 15 are equipment and a program for the methods.

## BSV relevance
The preferred embodiment is the bitcoin network and bitcoin nodes, including decisions about signing, mining, block validation and serving transactions (including OP_RETURN content). That is relevant to BSV node policy, SPV wallets and any service that filters transactions by compact membership tests.
