## Core thesis
A short Medium excerpt; the body continues on craigwright.net. First instalment of a memoir series reconstructing Craig's 2012 business history: Panopticrypt operating since 2011, a September 2012 consolidation of overseas assets and Australian-developed intellectual property, and late-2012 meetings with named associates — including Dave Kleiman by video link — all framed as record-digging driven by "the existing cases".

## Key arguments and claims
- Timeline claim: "Panopticrypt had been running since 2011", and by 2012 he "was ready to start moving some of my research into the mainstream".
- Corporate structure: "I'll detail Strassan PL in a later post, as it was fairly separate to Hotwire and related companies" — signalling a multi-entity structure to be unpacked across the series.
- September 2012: he began "consolidating assets that I had in a number of overseas companies and intellectual property that I was developing within Australia".
- On using multiple law firms then and now: "Problems generally occur when you put all your eggs in one basket for services."
- Records caveat: "I don't have all the records that I used to have, and it's been quite some years, but we have been digging through old records because of the existing cases" — the litigation context is the explicit motive for the reconstruction.
- The late-2012 meetings: Steven McLaughlin, "a friend that I'd come to know through Charles Sturt University" (where Craig was both a postgraduate student and a lecturer, and Steven had taken his courses); also present were "Jamie Wilson, Robert Urquhart, Dave Kleiman over a video link, and my lawyers who were dialed in, too".

## How Craig reasons (his model/logic)
The method is affidavit-style autobiography: named witnesses, dated events, and specific corporate entities (Panopticrypt, Hotwire, Strassan PL) assembled into a documentary record. He hedges on the completeness of records while asserting the broad strokes confidently, and openly frames the exercise as evidence-gathering for ongoing litigation rather than mere reminiscence.

## Where this contradicts BTC-mainstream logic
No direct engagement with crypto orthodoxy — this is personal-history testimony, not protocol argument. Its indirect function is to corroborate his claimed pre-2013 Bitcoin footprint and Satoshi-era timeline against sceptics, and to put his version of the 2012 corporate history (including Dave Kleiman's role) on the public record amid the Kleiman litigation.
